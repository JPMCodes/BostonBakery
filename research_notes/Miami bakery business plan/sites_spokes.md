## 3. Kiosks, carts and small in-line units at Dadeland Mall, The Falls, Shops at Merrick Park, CocoWalk and Brickell City Centre

### Takeaway
None of the five centres publishes a kiosk or cart rate. The only Miami-specific figure is a **Dadeland jewelry kiosk business for sale whose rent "is 5,000/month"**. Simon (Dadeland, The Falls and, since 2025, Brickell City Centre retail) and Brookfield/GGP (Merrick Park) both handle carts and kiosks through specialty leasing. The usual form is a license agreement, month to month or seasonal, rather than a lease. Generic guides put US mall kiosks at about $1,500–$15,000 a month, with food percentage rent of about 6–10% of sales. Dadeland's food court already has Auntie Anne's, Cinnabon and Häagen-Dazs, and ChurroMania returned to the food court in late 2025.

## 5. Pop-up storefronts: Coral Gables' city-owned 290 Miracle Mile programme and similar programmes

### Takeaway
290 Miracle Mile is an 850 sq ft city-owned storefront, offered "as-is" on a **6-month term with a 6-month renewal at the city's discretion, for $2,200 a month (about $31.06/sq ft), with electricity, water, HVAC and maintenance included**. The deposit is the first month plus two months' security ($6,600 up front, calc). The 2026 call closed on **May 15, 2026**, and Cabana Chic, LLC holds it from **Aug 29, 2026 to Feb 28, 2027**. The programme targets home décor, gifts, beauty and apparel. Food is not among the listed categories ("other unique, innovative uses" is the only opening), and the as-is space has no kitchen. The separate holiday sidewalk pop-up programme says "Food vendors are not allowed", except case by case for existing Miracle Mile food businesses. No comparable public programme was found in South Miami, Coconut Grove or the City of Miami. Miami Beach has one, from 2025.
