### Inferences
- **The best-earning box-celebration format earns about 21–22% four-wall EBITDA with about 76% gross margin.** That is unusually high for food. It comes from a narrow menu (one cake in several sizes), pre-ordered and gifting occasions, and premium decorated tiers: about $11 more for "decorated" over plain on the same cake, per ThePricer's OLD prices.
- **Price ladder:** bundtlet about $4–5, dozen bundtinis $18.50–$27, 8-inch $18.50–$29.50, 10-inch $28.50–$39.50.
  - The client's single roll at $5.25 sits at the top of the bundtlet band.
  - The 12-box at $50 sits near Nothing Bundt's 12-bundtlet box ($45–$54.50).
  - A "roll cake" would be compared with the $28–$40 10-inch bundt.
- **Average versus median matters.** The 2022 median ($974k) was about 34% below the mean (my arithmetic, using the 2022 median against the FY2023 mean of $1.48M; the years differ). A new independent should plan on median-type volumes or below.

  - "61 Crumbl locations have closed in 2026" (a fan site, cited via) — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91631321/crumbl-cookies-closing-stores-2026-list-shuttered-locations-list) [S, 2026].

  - A $1.99 value combo was introduced alongside closures — [lexpress-franchise](https://lexpress-franchise.com/en-us/news/crumbl-199-value-combo-store-closures-2026/) [S; title only].
- **Miami box prices:** not found. National 2026 aggregators (4-pack about $16.49, 6-pack $22.49–$28.99, 12-pack $35.99–$51.49) are in the earlier demand note.

### Inferences
- **Crumbl is the warning case for a novelty box-treat brand.** Volumes fell from $1.8M to $1.14M in three years. Median profit ($77k) was under a third of the average. Traffic fell 32%.
- **The rotating-flavour drop model brought trial, but not durable repeat visits once the novelty faded.** That is my reading, not a stated cause.
- **For the client:** occasion and gifting demand (Nothing Bundt's niche) looks steadier than novelty demand (Crumbl's). Nothing Bundt's 2023 average was $1.48M against Crumbl's 2025 $1.14M (different years).

### Gaps
- Status of the S Dixie Hwy listing and of Crumbl Kendall in Nov 2026.
- Miami box prices.
- Which Florida stores closed.

## 5. Misha's Cupcakes: closure, timeline, model, who picks up its customers

### Takeaway
