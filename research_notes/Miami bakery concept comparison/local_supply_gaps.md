## 1. Who trades in each category within about 15 minutes of Pinecrest, South Miami and Coral Gables (address, signature price, busyness)

### Takeaway
Coverage is very uneven across categories:
- **Well served or crowded:** Cuban bakeries, French-style croissant cafés (earlier notes), Basque cheesecake, custom celebration cakes made to order, and ice-cream and dessert substitutes.
- **Bagels:** went from almost none to crowded within 2026. H&H opened in Pinecrest in Feb 2026, Call Your Mother is due on Miracle Mile in early Dec 2026, and Mitch's is pending in Coral Gables.
- **Thin or absent inside the three towns:**
  - kosher and Jewish bakeries (Zak the Baker is in Wynwood);
  - Korean, Japanese and Chinese bakeries;
  - Argentine and Brazilian bakeries;
  - American-style biscuits, scones and coffee cake;
  - dedicated pie shops;
  - dedicated vegan or gluten-free bakeries (one of each, both on the Coral Gables edge).
