How to read these notes:
- **[S]** means the fact comes from search-result text only. I tried one page fetch (Miami New Times, Best of Miami 2026); the host would not resolve in this environment, so I did not try again and worked from search summaries. The search tool often merges several results into one summary. Where a quote could not be tied to one URL, I say "attribution unclear".
- Every fact carries a date or "undated". **OLD** marks anything from before 2025.
- "My arithmetic" marks a calculation from cited figures, not a published number.
- **Not repeated here:** the two earlier notes already cover cinnamon-roll demand, Knaus, Keik, Casa Bake, Roll Model, Roll & Dough, box prices, holiday pre-orders (rosca, Thanksgiving, dbakers), farmers markets, ezCater fees, hospital staff counts, the 3:05 cafecito ritual, closing-gift norms, fundraisers and delivery apps (`Miami roll house design/demand.md`, `Miami bakery business plan/channels.md`). I cite them as "earlier note" where they are needed for context.
- I used 35 web searches. **No Reddit thread surfaced in any search**; the tool returned press and listicles instead. The "what locals say" evidence below is therefore press, Infatuation reviews and directory text, not Reddit.

## 1. What locals say is missing or worth a drive (bread, bagels, croissants, American pastries, cakes, gluten-free, kosher, late-night, drive-through)

### Takeaway
- **Bread is the clearest stated gap.** Press repeats that Miami "just needs good bread". In the five target towns, sourdough comes mainly from pop-ups (Honey Veil) and a handful of cafés: Madruga, Facade and Bachour in Coral Gables.
- **Bagels are an old complaint,** and the most praised bagels (El Bagel, Bagel Bar East, Chesapeake in Aventura) are north of downtown, not in the southern suburbs.
- **Kosher:** no kosher bakery was found in Pinecrest, Kendall or South Miami. Zak the Baker (Wynwood) is the kosher reference.
- **Gluten-free:** dedicated gluten-free bakeries are few: Dora's in Coral Gables and an unnamed Kendall listing.
- **Late night:** what is open late is Cuban 24-hour bakeries (Pinecrest Bakery, Karla) and Crumbl, which stays open until midnight on weekends.
- **Drive-through:** no drive-through bakery was found in the area.

### Inferences
- **The strongest unmet categories the evidence supports are:**

## 2. Lines, sell-outs and viral items in Miami-Dade in 2025–26, and hype that faded

### Takeaway
- **What drew lines in 2025–26 was laminated or "Instagram" pastry and home bakeries,** mostly in Coconut Grove, Coral Gables and South Miami:
  - Ophelia's RV bakery in a Coconut Grove parking lot.
  - Sundays Croissanterie on Miracle Mile.
  - Dolce Isla, a Coral Gables home bakery (June 2026).
  - Flagler St. Bakery, downtown.
  - Keik's weekend rolls (earlier note).
  - Casa Bake (earlier note).

## 3. Celebration and gifting: spend per occasion, who captures it, custom cake prices in 2026

### Takeaway
- **Weddings:** The Knot's latest national survey puts the average wedding cake at about $530, and a 2025 Miami estimate is $496–$606 for 125 guests.
- **Quinceañeras:** national guides put the cake at $300–$800, or 3–5% of a party costing $15,000–$30,000.
- **Custom cakes in Miami** start at about $6.50–$10 a serving, often with a $200 minimum.
- **Publix,** the cheap default, charges about $39.99 for a decorated quarter sheet serving 15–20.
- **Nothing Bundt Cakes** has operated in Kendall and Pinecrest since 2012.
- **No Miami data was found** on spending for birthdays, baby showers, graduations or Mother's Day, or on Noche Buena and Three Kings dessert volumes.

### Inferences

## 6. Diet trends among affluent Miamians and their effect on bakery buying

### Takeaway
