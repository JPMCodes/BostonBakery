**My prior for the window, as odds:** 30% of design with probability 10%; 50% of design with probability 20%; 70% of design with probability 30%; 90% of design with probability 25%; 120% of design with probability 15%. Its mean is 74% of the design. This is judgment: the design's window equals an average Cinnaholic's whole year in 184 mornings.

- Expected Year 2 sales on that prior: $563,635.
- Signing the lease on that prior: expected Year 2 result $69,783, against the plan's $129,152 at 100%. Probability of clearing the $60,000 target comfortably (90% or more of design): 40%; of losing money in Year 2: 10%.
- With the window measured first and a lease signed only at 70% or better (probability 70%): expected Year 2 result if you sign $100,881.
- If the window comes in under 70% after the lease is signed and the kitchen closes after 18 months, the cash gone is about $303,593 at 30%; $242,837 at 50% (what was spent to open, less the deposit and 30% of the equipment's cost, less 18 months of results). Weighted by the prior, the test avoids an expected $78,927.
