## 1. US dedicated cinnamon-roll shops 2023–2026: openings, closures, multi-unit growth, sales; is there a boom and where is it in its cycle?

### Takeaway
The category is expanding, not fading, as of late 2026 — but the growth is concentrated in **Cinnabon's cheap, small-footprint express/co-branded units** (net +308 US units in 2025, ~30% growth), while the **dedicated sit-down specialist franchise (Cinnaholic) is flat at ~81–85 units with modest per-shop sales (~$350–375k)** and scattered closures. Independent roll-only shops are in a visible press/social boom (NYC, Houston, Miami/Broward pop-ups; Yelp search spikes in 2025–26). The pattern looks like the mid-to-late "viral" phase of a cycle: lots of new entrants and lines, plus early signs of operational strain (Roll & Dough's closure-to-restaff, mixed reviews), with no shake-out yet.

### Inferences
- **The durable money is in the Cinnabon model, not the specialist-café model.** Cinnabon's mall bakery averages ~$665k (median ~$600k) and is growing via tiny co-branded counters; Cinnaholic, a dedicated roll café with toppings, averages ~$350–375k and has barely grown in three years. A cinnamon-roll-only storefront should plan on Cinnaholic-type volumes, not Crumbl/Nothing Bundt volumes.
- **Cycle position (my judgment):** late-rise/peak of the social-media phase for independents. Indicators: NYT-style trend pieces in NYC (Feb 2026), founders explicitly invoking Crumbl, Yelp search spikes, pop-ups in many cities. No 2026 wave of independent roll-shop closures was found yet, but Cinnaholic's scattered closures and Roll & Dough's restaff-closure (Q3) show the operating strain.
- Unlike cupcakes, the cinnamon roll has a 40-year proven base (Cinnabon) and strong breakfast/holiday occasions, so the floor is higher than for a pure novelty.

## 2. What separates the survivors from the fads?

### Takeaway
Survivors pair the roll with an **everyday, low-ticket, impulse or ritual occasion** (Cinnabon: malls, airports, travel centers, Christmas Eve; Knaus: an annual Redland pilgrimage at ~$1.45 a roll; Scandinavian bun shops: daily fika with coffee) and add **beverages, co-brands or a broader menu**. Fads (Crumbs, Sprinkles, Crumbl) were single-item, premium-priced novelties that expanded fast on hype; when novelty faded, traffic fell.

### Cited Findings
- **Crumbs (2014):** collapsed largely from "growing too fast"; Magnolia's spokeswoman said the lesson was the danger of "focusing too heavily on one product, as well as expanding too quickly" — [Marketplace, Jul 8, 2014](https://www.marketplace.org/story/2014/07/08/cupcake-crumbles-lessons-crumbs-demise), [HeraldNet: "Crumbs closure shows focusing on one product can be risky"](https://www.heraldnet.com/business/crumbs-closure-shows-focusing-on-one-product-can-be-risky) [S; **OLD**].
  - Inc.: the fad-dessert cycle "is getting shorter and shorter" — [Inc.](https://www.inc.com/jill-krasny/lessons-from-crumbs-going-under.html) [S; **OLD**].
  - NPD: restaurant cake servings (incl. cupcake shops) fell 1% in the 12 months to Apr 2014 vs. +8% in 2011 — [FoodNavigator-USA](https://www.foodnavigator-usa.com/Article/2014/07/10/NPD-Group-on-Crumbs-closure-sliding-cupcake-sales) [S; **OLD**].
  - Outcome conflicts (Chapter 7 talk vs. Chapter 11 and later buyout) — [TIME](https://time.com/2978333/crumbs-bankruptcy-investors/) [S; **OLD**].
- **Sprinkles (the original viral cupcake shop) closed all company-owned stores Dec 31, 2025**, after 20 years; private-equity owned since 2012 (KarpReilly); no clear reason; one report quotes "financial conditions due to unforeseen business circumstances"; staff given one day's notice — [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/sprinkles-cupcakes-shutters-all-company-locations), [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/sprinkles-cupcakes-closes-company-owned-locations-nationwide/808673/), [Time Out, Jan 2, 2026](https://timeout.com/usa/news/once-uber-popular-sprinkles-cupcakes-has-officially-shut-down-010226), [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/retail/sprinkles-cupcakes-permanently-closing-all-stores-20-years-operation-founder-says) [S].
- **Crumbl** (earlier notes): AUV $1.8M (2022) → $1.14M (2025); traffic −32% (Aug 2026); ~61 closures in 2026.
- **Cinnabon's occasion model:** holiday sales run ~40% above baseline, especially Black Friday and Christmas week; multi-roll packs make up the majority of Christmas Eve sales; Christmas Eve is the single busiest day; catering rises — [Snack and Bakery](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/115035-cinnabon-rolls-out-sweet-bakery-innovations-year-round), [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/food/cinnabons-aroma-spreads-holiday-spirit-along-new-menu-items) [S; attribution unclear between the two; 2014 figure was ~50% — [TravelPulse](https://www.travelpulse.com/news/destinations/travel-gluttony-cinnabon-expects-huge-thanksgiving-holiday-sales), **OLD**]. Adds coffee (Seattle's Best, 2026) and co-brands (Q1).
- **Price as a survival factor:** Cinnabon Classic ~$5.29–$5.49 (earlier notes); Knaus ~$1.45; Keik $8.99–$9.99. Cinnaholic sits at the premium/topping end and has the weakest unit growth (Q1).
- **Scandinavian bun shops:** In the SF Bay Area cardamom buns "have become the most popular item at some bakeries"; Kantine sells 2–3× more cardamom knots than any other pastry — [SF Standard, Jul 10, 2026](https://sfstandard.com/2026/07/10/floral-swedish-pastry-dethroning-cinnamon-roll-bay-area-bakeries/) [S]. Fabrique Bakery (NYC) credited with popularizing the cardamom bun — [Taste](https://tastecooking.com/the-swedish-cardamom-bun-is-the-best-bun/) [S]. These sit inside full bakeries with coffee, not roll-only shops.
- **Decades-old institutions:** Knaus has run 70 seasons (2026–27 is the 70th) on a single low-priced, seasonal, cash-and-line model (Q3).

### Inferences
- **Survivor traits (my synthesis):** low or fair unit price; a recurring ritual occasion (commute/travel, Sunday breakfast, Christmas morning, annual farm trip); coffee attached; scarcity managed by season or limits rather than by hype; slow growth funded by cash flow. **Fad traits:** premium single-item novelty, rotating "drops" to sustain buzz, rapid multi-unit or PE-funded expansion, showy leases.
- A roll-led bakery is safest positioned as a **breakfast/holiday-box bakery with coffee and 2–4 sister doughs** (babka, cardamom bun, pan de jamón season, rosca) rather than a "roll-only, many-toppings" shop.
- Gift/pre-order share for Cinnabon is high at Christmas but low the rest of the year (my inference from "multi-packs dominate Christmas Eve").

### Gaps
- Published shares of pre-orders/gifts for any roll seller; Crumbl's gifting share; Scandinavian bun shop economics.

## 3. Sales mix and seasonality of roll shops

### Takeaway
Hard data are scarce and come almost entirely from Cinnabon: **~40% above baseline in holiday weeks, Christmas Eve the peak day, multi-packs the majority of Christmas Eve sales, some stores taking ~500 Christmas-morning pre-orders**. Search interest peaks Nov–Jan and dips in spring/summer. Miami's best-known roll sellers (Knaus, Keik) are explicitly seasonal (Oct–May; fall weekends).

### Cited Findings
- Cinnabon holiday lift ~40%, Black Friday and Christmas week; Christmas Eve busiest day; multi-packs majority of Christmas Eve sales; seasonal staff and longer hours; "some stores receive 500 preorders for sweet treats on Christmas morning" — [Snack and Bakery](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/115035-cinnabon-rolls-out-sweet-bakery-innovations-year-round), [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/food/cinnabons-aroma-spreads-holiday-spirit-along-new-menu-items) [S; the 500 pre-orders figure could not be tied to one URL — attribution unclear].
- Thanksgiving travel is a Cinnabon peak (airport units) — [TravelPulse](https://www.travelpulse.com/news/destinations/travel-gluttony-cinnabon-expects-huge-thanksgiving-holiday-sales) [S; **OLD**].
- Cinnaholic runs holiday menus each year — [Snack and Bakery](https://www.snackandbakery.com/articles/114715-cinnaholic-announces-holiday-flavors) [S; undated].
- Search interest: low spring–summer 2025, rising Aug, peaks Nov 2025 and Jan 2026 — [Accio](https://www.accio.com/business/cinnamon_rolls_trend) [S; low-quality source].
- Pastry Arts: classic flavor outsells other flavors 3:1 at one roll bakery — [Pastry Arts](https://pastryartsmag.com/trends/on-a-roll-long-a-pastry-staple-the-cinnamon-roll-is-moving-to-center-stage/) [S, 2025].
- Roll & Dough ran Thursday–Sunday hours only (noon–6 Thu–Sat, 9–2 Sun) at its Miramar pop-up — [TikTok @therollanddough](https://www.tiktok.com/@therollanddough/video/7626613826804272397), [Yelp, updated Sept 2026](https://www.yelp.com/biz/roll-and-dough-miramar) [S].
- Mother's Day and summer roll-shop data: not found.

### Inferences
- Plan for Q4 (Thanksgiving week through Christmas Eve) and January as the box/pre-order peak, a smaller Mother's Day bump (unproven), and a summer trough — consistent with Knaus closing May–October and Keik running rolls only in fall.
- The 3:1 classic-flavor ratio argues for a narrow menu (classic + 2–3 rotating), which also lowers waste.

### Gaps
- Box vs. single share, pre-order share, and monthly sales curve for any independent roll shop; Mother's Day and summer data.

## 5. Neighboring sweet-dough products: which have demand evidence in South Florida and which are growing nationally?

### Takeaway
Strongest **South Florida** evidence: **pan de jamón** (December, $30–$38.50, sell-outs — earlier notes), **rosca/roscón de Reyes** ($18–$35, January — earlier notes), **golfeados** (stable Doral shops at roll-shop prices), **brioche doughnuts** (The Salty, 10 years), and now **babka** (Bakey's July 2026 entry with expansion plans). Strongest **national growth**: **Korean/Japanese milk-bread bakery cafés** (Paris Baguette, Tous les Jours, both targeting ~1,000 units), **cardamom buns** (SF/NYC, out-selling cinnamon rolls at some bakeries), **conchas/pan dulce** (trade forecasts for 2026), and **kolaches** via Shipley (17–18 straight quarters of positive sales, South Florida deal).

### Inferences
- **Best complements (my ranking for south Miami-Dade):** (1) pan de jamón (December) and rosca de Reyes (January) — proven local willingness to pay $18–$38 for a celebration bread, landing right after the roll's Thanksgiving–Christmas peak; (2) babka/twist loaves as gift loaves — a new chain entrant validates demand but also adds a competitor; (3) a cardamom or guava-cardamom bun as the "everyday, less sweet" item with coffee; (4) golfeado-style roll to tap the Venezuelan base at the same price ladder.
- Milk-bread sweet buns are a **threat more than an opportunity**: chains like Paris Baguette will price them well below an independent.
- Kolaches/savory rolls (ham-and-cheese, croqueta-filled) could lift weekday breakfast traffic, but Shipley's South Florida build-out could crowd that lane.
