# Verification batch 2: Boston-area bakery site candidates

Checked 2026-11-07. 20 candidates from the sweeps (`sweep_suburb-lease.md`, `sweep_business-for-sale.md`, `sweep_vacated-food-spaces.md`). Web searches used: **15 of 15**.

Evidence comes from four places:

- **Search-result snippets.** Listing sites (LoopNet, Crexi, CityFeet, BizBuySell, BizQuest and similar) block direct fetches, so their details are snippet-based. The search tool summarizes snippets, and those summaries can attach a figure to the wrong listing.
- **Boston Restaurant Talk (BRT).** I searched BRT's full-content feed (2025-10-15 to 2026-11-06, downloaded by the vacated-spaces sweep) and its own blog search.
- **boston.com.** One MLS page and one article.
- **data.boston.gov.** Food-license and inspection records.

Fit scores follow the batch rubric: size 0-2, rent or deal 0-2, second-generation food space 0-3, location 0-2, status 0-1. Annual rent is size x rent, and sales needed is annual rent / 0.08. -1 means not stated.
