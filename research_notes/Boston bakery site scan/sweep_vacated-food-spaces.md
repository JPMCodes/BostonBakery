# Vacated food spaces sweep: second-generation bakery/cafe sites

Scan date: 2026-11-07. Scope: food spaces (bakeries, cafes, coffee shops, small restaurants) that closed roughly November 2025 to November 2026 in Boston neighborhoods, Cambridge, Somerville, Brookline and the inner suburbs named in the brief, whether or not a listing has appeared. Target: 900-3,000 SF, $30-55/SF/yr, second-generation food space with crowds beyond weekday commuters.

Fit is a first-pass judgment. **A** = former bakery, cafe or food-ready restaurant in a target area, closed in the window, no re-let found. **B** = partial match (restaurant rather than cafe, size or rent unknown or likely high, closure only from licence data, or closed just before the window). **C** = weak (likely taken, redevelopment, prime rent, commuter location, too large or small, or a licence-data lead with no news). "-" means not stated (the structured output uses -1). "Basis" says whether details came from reading the page or data (page_read), only from search snippets (snippet_only), or both (mixed). For Boston addresses, "City licence check" is from the City of Boston food-inspection, active-licence and licensing-board datasets (refreshed 2026-11-06).

## Method and access notes

- Boston Restaurant Talk: the Blogger JSON feed was downloaded with curl: 672 posts published 2025-10-15 to 2026-11-06 (189 labelled "restaurant closings", plus "closings" and "bar closings"). BRT has no monthly closings post; its weekly "Restaurant News for the Week Ending..." posts repeat its own stories. Every candidate name was also searched across all 672 posts to catch later re-let posts ("... Opens in the Former X Space"). WebFetch of bostonrestaurants.blogspot.com was not needed; curl worked.
- BRT's sister list (hiddenboston.com/closings-openings.html) failed with a DNS error in WebFetch (blocked by the network policy) and was not retried.
- Eater Boston: /closings and /restaurant-news were read with curl. The monthly closings roundups stop after January 2026; the November 2025, December 2025 and January 2026 editions were read.
- Universal Hub: curl got a Cloudflare "Attention Required" 403 (the site's own bot protection) and WebFetch failed DNS; neither was retried. Universal Hub items were reached only through BRT citations and search snippets.
- Boston.com: restaurant-closings tag pages 1-9 (back to late 2024) and seven articles were read (Party Favors/Golden Temple, Nine Winters, Belle's, Naco Taco, Cafe Barada, home.stead, and the Oct 26, 2026 Starbucks closures piece). Boston.com site search and the starbucks tag page returned nothing useful.
- data.boston.gov (CKAN): package_search found Food Establishment Inspections (907,582 rows), Active Food Establishment Licenses (3,360 rows) and Licensing Board Licenses (3,638 rows, including Common Victualler premises descriptions with square footage and seats), all refreshed 2026-11-06. The two smaller tables were downloaded in full. Inspections were queried with datastore_search_sql by address and business name (about 95 queries). A vacancy screen listed licences in target Boston ZIPs that went Inactive with an inspection since 2025-08 (111) and flagged the 42 with no active food licence at the same street number and name.
- Licence-data caveats: shops that closed in 2026 often still show Active until year-end renewal (home.stead, Lulu Green, Deli One, Chip City Causeway). Inactive can also mean an ownership change with a new licence number. CV descriptions reflect the premises when licensed, not today.
- Cross-check: candidate addresses were matched against the boston.com listing cards collected by the lease sweeps (Boston rentals, suburban sales/rentals, business opportunities): no matches. Overlaps with the suburb-lease and Boston-lease sweeps are noted per row.
- Downloads: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-BostonBakery/f936cb80-2548-5c6e-84ff-5b8b8418bc02/scratchpad/downloads/vacated_brt, vacated_eater, vacated_bostoncom, vacated_databoston. Scripts: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user-BostonBakery/f936cb80-2548-5c6e-84ff-5b8b8418bc02/scratchpad/vacated_scripts.
