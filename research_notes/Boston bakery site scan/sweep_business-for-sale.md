# Business-for-sale sweep: Greater Boston bakeries, cafes and food-ready kitchens

Scan date: 2026-11-07. Scope: businesses for sale in Boston, Cambridge, Somerville, Brookline and the inner suburbs on the criteria list that are, or could become, a bakery: bakeries, bakery-cafes, cafes, coffee shops, donut/bagel shops and small restaurants with a kitchen. Target: 900-3,000 SF, rent $30-55/SF/yr and at most about 8% of sales, second-generation food space, transferable license, 5+ years of lease including options. Verifier still needs to confirm each listing is live, the lease terms, the hood/grease trap and the license transfer with the broker.

Fit column is a first-pass judgment: **A** = bakery or cafe business in the target area with an asking price plus stated financials (or size and rent); **B** = partial (missing price or financials, conflicting snippets, town uncertain, small size, or high rent); **C** = weak (stale, outside the target towns, real-estate-heavy, not bakery-like, or title only). In the tables, "-" means not stated (the structured output uses -1). Weekly sales were annualized x52 and say so in the notes. "Basis" is page_read for boston.com MLS pages read directly and snippet_only for everything seen only through search snippets. The search tool returns summaries of result snippets, and those summaries sometimes attach a price to the wrong listing; conflicts are flagged in the notes.

## Method and access notes

- No direct fetches of BizBuySell, BizQuest, BusinessesForSale, LoopNet or other listing sites (they return 403 to bots); all of their details are snippet-based. The search tool returns a model-written summary of result snippets, which sometimes pairs a figure with the wrong listing; where two searches disagreed, both figures are recorded.
- WebFetch failed with DNS errors (outside the session's network allowlist) for businessesforsale.nebba.com and www.baystatebusinessbrokers.com (BayState's own page for the Irish bakery listing). After the second failure no further new domains were tried, so openfair.co, corbetthub.com, vantagepointretail.com, 909brokers.com and realmo.com were seen only through snippets. Allowing www.baystatebusinessbrokers.com and vantagepointretail.com would let a verifier read those broker pages.
- boston.com (MLS PIN feed) worked through curl: commercial-for-sale search (channel=sales, propertyType=Commercial, newest first) paged for freetext Boston (16 pages), Cambridge (2), Somerville (1), Brookline (1) and 17 suburbs (Newton, Quincy, Medford, Watertown, Arlington, Belmont, Melrose, Winchester, Lexington, Needham, Wellesley, Dedham, Burlington, Hingham, Lynnfield, Malden, Everett): 228 unique cards, filtered locally for food uses; 6 detail pages read (73495863, 73502637, 73530259, 73581817, 73441429, 73580699); 73530535 is from its search card only. 1.5 s spacing, no errors. Freetext matching also returns out-of-town properties on streets with the town's name. Files: `/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-BostonBakery/f936cb80-2548-5c6e-84ff-5b8b8418bc02/scratchpad/downloads/bfs_bostoncom/`.
- boston.com "Date Added" can be the date a listing entered the feed (many show 2026-09-03), so treat it as "on market since at least" that date.
- Search budget: 36 web searches were run, one over the 35-search cap, because of a counting slip noticed only at the end. No search returned a quota error.
