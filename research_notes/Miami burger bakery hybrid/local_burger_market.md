## 1. Burger sellers within about 15 minutes of Pinecrest, South Miami and Coral Gables: format, prices, hours, busyness

### Takeaway
- **Within 15 minutes:** a dense mix.
  - Chains: Shake Shack, Pincho, BurgerFi, Checkers, Habit at Cutler Bay, Five Guys.
  - Bar-and-grills: Keg South, Sports Grill, Flanigan's.
  - Upscale: The Capital Burger.
  - New smash and craft independents that arrived in 2025–2026: Skinny Louie South Miami, La Birra Bar Miracle Mile, Lala's Kendall, Chuggie's Grove, Star Burger on SW 8th St.
  - A butcher-counter burger: Babe's, Palmetto Bay.
- **The most-awarded names are mostly outside the 15-minute ring:** Cowy, Ted's, USBS, Blue Collar, Burgermeister.
- **Not in the area:** Wahlburgers, Smashburger and Wayback.
- **Price structure:**
  - Value smash single: about $8 (Chuggie's $7.95).
  - Fast-casual chains: about $11–15 for a single cheeseburger on delivery apps.
  - Bar-and-grills: about $11–12.
  - Butcher and craft: about $17.

## 2. Openings and closures 2024–2026; empty spaces; what the smash-burger pop-ups became

### Takeaway
2025–2026 brought several smash and craft independents into the area:
- Chuggie's, Grove (Oct 2025).
- Lala's, Kendall (Dec 2025/Jan 2026).
- Skinny Louie, South Miami (Feb 2026).
- La Birra Bar, Miracle Mile (Jun 2026).
- Star Burger, SW 8th St (2026).
- Babe's, Palmetto Bay: 40-seat expansion (Mar 2026).

The notable closure is Lokal, Coconut Grove, on June 28, 2026, after 15 years. The owner cited a 50% rent increase and costly building repairs.

BurgerFi went through Chapter 11 in Sept 2024, with nine corporate units closed. No Miami-specific closure list was found.

The 2024–2026 pop-up pattern runs one way: weekly pop-up, then brewery or BBQ residency, then a permanent shop. Cowy, Ted's, USBS and Lala's all took that path; Skinny Louie took it and then grew to eight units. Most landed in Wynwood or Little River rather than the south suburbs. Lala's is the exception.

## 4. Breakfast sandwiches: who, prices, availability of an American egg sandwich on a fresh roll, early hours

### Takeaway
Breakfast sandwiches in the area come from three places:
- the Cuban ventanita and cafeteria tradition: pan con tortilla, or ham, egg and cheese on Cuban bread, about $6.50–$11;
- premium bakeries (Bachour and Madruga in Coral Gables) and H&H's B.E.C. in Pinecrest;
- the Chug's hybrid in the Grove.

**An American egg sandwich on a house-baked roll exists but only at a few premium bakery-cafés.** The citywide favourite of 2026, Ophelia, is a bakery trailer that sells out by 11 a.m. That points to demand outrunning supply for the premium version.

## 6. Delivery: how burgers rank, 2026 commission tiers, typical markups

### Takeaway
DoorDash's 2026 published commission is 15% (Basic), 25% (Plus) or 30% (Premier) of the delivery subtotal. Uber Eats is reported in a similar 15–30% range, but no official rate card was found. All-in costs are reported at 25–40%.

Local burger shops mark up delivery menus, with about 30% cited as typical. No Miami city-level ranking of burger delivery orders was found. DoorDash's 2024 data says chicken burgers were Florida's most-ordered burger type.

### Cited Findings
- **DoorDash 2026 merchant pricing:** Basic 15%, Plus 25%, Premier 30% of subtotal on delivery orders — [DoorDash merchants](https://merchants.doordash.com/llms.txt/pricing) [S].
- **Pickup commission:** 6%, reported by one blog that could not confirm the higher tiers — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/doordash-vs-ubereats/) [S, lower confidence].
- **Uber Eats:** 15–30%, with higher tiers buying visibility; third-party estimate only — [Unplug Dining](https://unplugdining.com/blog/the-true-cost-of-doordash-and-uber-eats-commissions-in-2026) [S, 2026].
- **Effective total cost:** about 25–35% including marketing and processing — [Fleksa](https://fleksa.com/en/cost/online-ordering-fees) [S]; 30–40% as reported by owners — [Sauce](https://www.getsauce.com/post/delivery-app-commission-rates-restaurants) [S].
- **Large-chain rates:** large chains negotiate lower rates — [Duck Hub](https://www.duck-hub.com/blog/food-delivery-commission-rates) [S].
- **Typical delivery-menu markup:** Burger Beast advises that on third-party delivery "expect the prices to be about 30% more" — search summary of [Burger Beast](https://burgerbeast.com/?p=684045) [S].
- **Sports Grill Bird Rd:** Sports Burger $11.25 in one listing and $18.75 in a "featured" listing on the ordering site — [order.online](https://order.online/store/-74471/) [S; the gap may reflect a combo or markup, unclear].
- **Delivery and pickup prices may differ:** Cowy's Toast and DoorDash pages say merchant-set prices can differ by channel — [DoorDash](https://www.doordash.com/store/cowy-burger-24615387/) [S]. Pincho's listing says the same [S].
- **Florida's most-ordered burger type:** DoorDash's 2024 orders show chicken burgers most popular in 17 states, including Florida — [AOL](https://www.aol.com/no-1-burger-state-according-163100272.html) [S, OLD].
- **"Most Ordered" sections:** BurgerFi Collins Ave lists its cheeseburger as the top item — [order.online](https://order.online/store/-26209694/) [S].
- **DoorDash's Miami burger guide (2023):** Cheeseburger Baby, Lokal, Kush's frita, Al Carbon. It is editorial, not volume-based — [DoorDash blog](https://blog.doordash.com/en-us/post/best-burgers-in-miami) [S, OLD].

### Inferences
- **Delivery economics:** with a 25–30% commission and about a 30% menu markup, burger shops roughly pass the fee through to the customer. A $12 in-store double would list at about $15.50 on the apps.
- **Delivery-app price comparisons are unreliable:** the chains' app prices (Shake Shack double about $12–14, Five Guys cheeseburger about $14.50–15) are inflated relative to in-store.

### Gaps
- An official Uber Eats US commission card.
- Miami- or ZIP-level rankings of burger delivery.
- In-store and app price pairs for any one south-suburb burger shop.
