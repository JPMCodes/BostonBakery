# Unit Economics of Small Counter-Service Burger Shops (US, 2025-2026) and What an In-House Bakery Adds

Research date: November 25, 2026. Method note: about 36 web searches were run. Direct page fetches failed in this environment (the hosts restaurantbusinessonline.com and jackintheboxfranchising.com did not resolve), so per the brief I did not retry and worked from search-result text. Every figure below therefore comes from search-result snippets or summaries, not from reading the full pages or FDDs. Figures from franchise aggregators (Sharpsheets, Zeereport, Pulse RevOps, franchiseinvestordata, Franzy) are lower-reliability. Some of them are AI-generated, and several cite FDD years that cannot exist yet (for example a "2027 FDD").

## 1. Sales per shop and investment (chains, small smash-burger chains, independents)

### Takeaway
Better-burger franchise units typically do about $0.9M to $1.9M a year. MOOYAH (about $1.1M average, $1.0M median, FY2025) and Killer Burger (about $1.6M) are the closest analogs to a small counter shop with no drive-through. Five Guys runs about $1.5M and Freddy's about $1.8M to $1.9M, but Freddy's sites usually have a drive-through. Full franchise build-outs cost about $0.33M to $2.3M. Independent burger counters listed for sale in Miami report about $0.6M to $0.8M in revenue and roughly $100K to $160K in owner cash flow.

### Inferences

- **Owner cash flow:** About 12% to 25% of sales appears in listings. Seller "cash flow" (SDE) usually includes the owner's pay.
- **Drive-through effect:** Freddy's and Habit volumes are boosted by drive-throughs, so they overstate what a walk-up shop can do.

## 2. Cost shares for a counter-service burger shop

### Takeaway
For limited-service restaurants generally, the 2024 medians (NRA, published 2025) are:
- food 32.4%
- labor 31.7%
- prime cost about 65%
- occupancy 5.2% (6.0% in urban sites)
- pre-tax profit 4.0%

Burger concepts run food and paper around 29% to 32% because of beef. Third-party delivery takes 15% to 30% commissions.

### Cited Findings
- **NRA limited-service medians (2024 data, 2025 abstract):**
  - Food and beverage 32.4%; labor and benefits 31.7%. Labor was 30.0% for profitable operators and 34.1% for loss-making ones.
  - Prime cost was about 65 cents per sales dollar, and pre-tax income a median of 4.0%.
  - [NRA food cost](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/restaurant-operators-kept-food-cost-ratios-in-check-in-2024/); [NRA labor](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/elevated-labor-costs-had-a-significant-impact-on-restaurant-profitability-in-2024/)
- **NRA occupancy (limited-service):** 5.2% overall, 6.0% urban, 5.0% suburban, 3.2% small town or rural. [NRA occupancy](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/restaurant-occupancy-costs-were-more-than-5-of-sales-in-2024/)
- **Volume and food cost:** Per the NRA, higher-volume restaurants reported lower food cost ratios in 2024. [NRA](https://restaurant.org/research-and-media/research/restaurant-economic-insights/analysis-commentary/higher-volume-restaurants-reported-lower-food-cost-ratios-in-2024/)
- **Shake Shack:**
  - Food and paper was 28.8% of Shack sales in Q2 2026, blamed on record beef prices. [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/shake-shack-q2-earnings-call-highlights)
  - Beef is about 30% of the food and paper basket. [GuruFocus transcript](https://www.gurufocus.com/stock/SHAK/transcripts/8658496)
- **Delivery commissions:**
  - One restaurateur says delivery apps take 15% to 30%. A burger operator negotiated down from a 25% ask and marks up app prices, so a $10 burger sells for $12. [Dallas Observer](https://www.dallasobserver.com/food-drink/why-dallas-restaurants-are-struggling-with-delivery-fees-40662755/)
  - Independents mark up delivery items by about $1.25 on average, and chains by $1.90 (Intouch Insight). [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/delivery-takeout-solutions/when-it-comes-to-third-party-delivery-not-all-experiences-are-created-equally)
- **Franchise royalties and marketing fees:** These add to costs in franchise models, for example Wayback 5% + 2% + 2% [Franchise Direct](https://www.franchisedirect.com/foodfranchises/wayback-burgers-franchise-07298/ufoc) and Smashburger 5.5% + 2.25% [Sharpsheets](https://sharpsheets.io/blog/smashburger-franchise-costs-fees-profits/).

### Inferences
- **Below about $1M of sales:** Fixed costs take a larger share as sales fall. These are minimum labor coverage (two to three people for every open hour), rent, insurance and utilities.
  - Example: on $700K of sales, a Miami rent of $70K to $90K is 10% to 13% of sales, about double the NRA 6% urban median.
  - Store-level margin can fall from 12% to 18% at $1.5M+ to the low single digits or below at $600K to $800K.
- **What an independent saves:** An independent avoids 7% to 9% of royalty and marketing fees, which partly offsets the lower volume.
- **Delivery-heavy mix:** With a 25% delivery mix at a 20% net commission, margin drops about 5 points unless app prices are marked up.

### Gaps
- **Burger-specific data:** No burger-specific breakdown of labor and occupancy by sales band was found, and no FDD cost tables (for example Freddy's or MOOYAH Item 19 cost lines) were seen.
- **Pre-2024 source:** The 2025 Citrin Cooperman benchmarking report was found but not read. [Issuu](https://issuu.com/citrincooperman/docs/2025_restaurant_industry_benchmarking_report)

## 3. Dayparts and mix (breakfast, delivery, desserts and shakes, average check)

### Takeaway
Hard figures are scarce:
- Third-party delivery is 20% to 30% of sales at many quick-service chains.
- At breakfast-led chains, breakfast runs about 45% to 48% of sales (Hardee's).
- At the Schlotzsky's-Cinnabon co-brands, treats rose from about 2% to 10% to 12% of sales.

No burger-chain shake attach rates or 2025 average checks were found.

### Cited Findings
- **Third-party delivery share:** AlixPartners estimates third-party orders at 20% to 30% of sales at many quick-service chains. [AlixPartners](https://www.alixpartners.com/insights/102mgom/qsrs-face-their-amazon-moment/)
- **Shake Shack digital share:** Digital (kiosk, app and web) reached 58.9% of Shack sales in one quarter. Third-party delivery is now tracked separately. [Fast Casual](https://www.fastcasual.com/articles/shake-shack-redraws-digital-line-around-kiosks/)
- **Hardee's breakfast:** Breakfast was about 45% of sales (older coverage) and 48% in 2010 (SEC filing recap). [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/menu-trends/hardee-s-bolsters-breakfast-menu)
- **Hardee's biscuits:** Biscuits are cited at almost 50% of sales in 2018 and about a third in 2020. These figures are inconsistent and come through a secondary outlet. [Mashed](https://www.mashed.com/975795/the-truth-about-hardees-biscuits)
- **Schlotzsky's with Cinnabon and Carvel (2010):** The average check rose from about $7 to $9.85, and treats rose from 1.5% to 2% of sales to 10% to 12%. [FES Magazine](https://fesmag.com/topics/the-latest-news/5874-president-kelly-roddy-chief-marketing-officer-greg-regian-vp-purchasing-matt-diana-vp-restaurant-development-jay-allen-vp-company-operations-and-training-george-schaefer-vp-franchise-development-sales-david-wheeler-vp-research-and-development)
- **Average check proxy:** 7th Street Burger prices are $6.50 for a cheeseburger, $9.50 for a double and $4.50 for fries. [Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/food/7th-street-burger-fishtown-opens-20260828.html)

### Inferences
- **Average check:** A burger, fries and drink at an independent smash counter likely runs $14 to $18 in 2026. This is an inference from 7th Street pricing plus a drink.
- **Dessert share:** A strong baked dessert could plausibly take 8% to 12% of sales, using the Schlotzsky's-Cinnabon precedent. It is an old (2010) and different-format data point.

### Gaps
- **Missing figures:** No 2025-2026 data was found on shake or dessert attach rates at burger chains (Freddy's custard share, Shake Shack shake mix) or on breakfast share at burger counters.
- **No average check:** No published average check for Five Guys, Smashburger or MOOYAH was found.

## 4. Beef and other inputs, late 2026

### Takeaway
Wholesale 81% lean ground beef was about $3.10 to $3.56 a pound, FOB the packer, in September and October 2026. That is down about $0.48 a pound from late October 2025, but still historically high, and retail ground beef hit a record $7.06 a pound in May 2026. The cattle herd is at a record low, Mexican cattle imports remain halted over screwworm, and 2027 forecasts show record or near-record cattle prices. Brazilian tariff relief and Argentine lean trimmings are the main offsets.

### Cited Findings
- **Wholesale 81% lean, September 2026:** USDA's daily boxed beef report for September 28, 2026 had negotiated 10-lb chub sales at a weighted average of $356.00/cwt (about $3.56 a pound), FOB. [Restaurant Launch](https://restaurantlaunch.com/answers/ground-beef-price-increase-how-much-to-raise-burger-price)
- **Wholesale 81% lean, late October 2026:**
  - It was about $3.14 a pound on the daily boxed beef list. [Meatingplace (Hales)](https://meatingplace.com/hales-beef-cutout-prices-slide-as-packers-weigh-slaughter-cuts/)
  - Forward sales (22 to 90 day delivery) were $313.75/cwt for 1.138M lb, which was $48.23 lower than the same week of 2025. [Meatingplace (Hales)](https://meatingplace.com/hales-beef-cutout-prices-slide-as-packers-weigh-slaughter-cuts/)
- **Retail ground beef:** It hit a record $7.06 a pound in May 2026, about 13% above a year earlier. [Transport Topics](https://www.ttnews.com/articles/beef-prices-hit-record-may)
- **Wholesale ground beef relative to cuts (mid-2026):** Wholesale ground beef reached about 32% of the ribeye price, the highest share since December 2015.   - Soybean oil was up about 37% year to date in H1 2026, driven by EPA biofuel policy.
  - Cash-settled cheese was down about 8% year to date.
  - [CME July 2026 Ag Index report](https://www.cmegroup.com/news/2026/july-2026-ags-index-report.html)

### Inferences
- **Patty cost:** At $3.10 to $3.56 a pound wholesale, raw beef for a 4-oz patty (one quarter-pound or two 2-oz smashes) costs about $0.78 to $0.89 FOB. Distributor markup and delivery likely add 15% to 30%, for about $0.95 to $1.15 delivered. A double is about $1.90 to $2.30 in beef alone.
- **Fresh-cut fries:** Fresh-cut fries from whole potatoes are likely cheaper per pound than frozen but cost more labor. No data on this was found.
- **2027 budget:** Plan for beef to stay flat to slightly higher in 2027, with downside only if consumer demand weakens.

### Gaps
- **Prices not found:** No foodservice (distributor-delivered) 80/20 chuck price, no current CME block cheddar or American cheese case price, and no fryer oil jug price.
- **Earnings-call coverage:** McDonald's and Wendy's 2026 commentary on beef costs was not captured.

## 6. Savory plus cinnamon roll: co-branding and baked desserts at burger shops

### Takeaway
Co-branding a sweet bakery item with a savory concept adds volume, but not the full sum of two stand-alone stores. Auntie Anne's-Cinnabon co-brands average about $1.2M against $0.67M to $0.96M for the stand-alone brands (GoTo Foods claims about 45% higher AUV). For Schlotzsky's-Cinnabon, the documented effect is treats at 10% to 12% of sales, a check up about 40%, and an afternoon (2 to 4 p.m.) lift. Whataburger and Runza both sell cinnamon rolls, but no sales data is public.

### Cited Findings
- **GoTo Foods (company claim):** Auntie Anne's-Cinnabon co-brands do about 45% higher AUV than stand-alone units. [QSR Web](https://www.qsrweb.com/articles/goto-foods-co-branding-renaissance-pairs-signature-brands/)
- **FDD-based AUVs:**

  | Format | AUV |
  |---|---|
  | Auntie Anne's enclosed-mall stand-alone | about $792K |
  | Auntie Anne's outlet center | about $958K |
  | Cinnabon franchised mall | about $665K |
  | Co-branded | about $1.2M |

  The co-brand is not fully additive: the two stand-alones sum to about $1.4M to $1.6M. [Restaurant Dive](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/wetzels-pretzels-cold-stone-dual-branded-franchising/820274/)
- **Why co-brand:** The parent says co-branding lets the combined sales support street-side rents. [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/outside-mall-future-cinnabon-auntie-annes-cobranding)
- **Cinnabon growth:** Cinnabon had a "growth eruption" in 2025 (headline only). [QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/story/cinnabon-had-a-growth-eruption-in-2025/)
- **Schlotzsky's with Cinnabon and Carvel:**
  - **2010:** The check rose from about $7 to $9.85 and treats from 1.5% to 2% of sales to 10% to 12%. [FES Magazine](https://fesmag.com/topics/the-latest-news/5874-president-kelly-roddy-chief-marketing-officer-greg-regian-vp-purchasing-matt-diana-vp-restaurant-development-jay-allen-vp-company-operations-and-training-george-schaefer-vp-franchise-development-sales-david-wheeler-vp-research-and-development)

  - **Cannibalization:** Executives said Cinnabon did not cannibalize the brand's own desserts. [QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com/news/schlotzskys-goes-all-cinnabon-new-look)

- **Whataburger:** It sells cinnamon rolls individually or in a tray of six, described as available 24 hours a day at all restaurants. [Whataburger Stories](https://stories.whataburger.com/on-a-roll-check-out-whataburgers-new-cinnamon-rolls/)

- **Sales lift:** A burger counter adding a signature in-house cinnamon roll could plausibly add 8% to 15% to sales. The lift would come mostly from new morning and afternoon occasions and take-home trays rather than burger attach.

## 7. State of the burger category, 2025-2026

### Takeaway
The burger segment is weak and splitting in two:
- **Leaders:** High-volume chains (In-N-Out, Culver's, Whataburger) and some better-burger chains (Freddy's, Shake Shack at +2% to +4%) are growing.
- **Strugglers:** Wendy's (same-store sales down about 7%, traffic down 12.5%, hundreds of closures), Jack in the Box (closures through 2028) and Five Guys (some closures) are not.
- **Pricing:** Value wars ($5 to $7 meal deals) coexist with premium "beefier" launches.
- **Smash burgers:** The format is crowded. UK closures are visible, but no hard US saturation data was found.
- **GLP-1 drugs:** Studies show users' fast-food spending falls by about 8%, while items per visit fall only about 1%.

### Cited Findings
- **Category growth:**
  - Burger chain sales averaged about 1.5% growth over two years (Technomic Top 1,500).
  - Chains with unit volumes above $2M outperformed.
  - Culver's grew 14% and In-N-Out 9.6%, while Jack in the Box fell 4.3% and Hardee's 5%.
  - [Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/financing/sluggish-burger-business-volumes-matter); [Restaurant Business Top 500](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/financing/technomic-top-500-chain-restaurant-sales-slowed-again-2025)
- **Wendy's:**
  - Q2 2026 system sales fell 8.2% and same-store sales deteriorated by 7%. Burger King passed Wendy's in US system sales ($3.2B in the quarter). [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/restaurant-finance/wendy-s-takes-steps-to-fix-its-brand-as-sales-fall-again)
  - It planned to close 5% to 6% of US units in 2026. [NRN](https://www.nrn.com/top-500-restaurants/wendy-s-to-close-hundreds-of-u-s-restaurants)
  - US same-store sales were down 7%, traffic down 12.5%, with 245 closures as of August 2026. [Eat This](https://www.eatthis.com/fast-food-chains-losing-customers-august-12-2026/)
- **Jack in the Box (fiscal Q3 2026):**
  - Same-store sales fell 1.1%, with 4 openings and 17 closures.
  - It closed 40 stores year to date and expects 10 to 20 more in Q4, with accelerated closures continuing into 2027 and 2028.
  - [Business Wire](https://secure.businesswire.com/news/home/20260812600878/en/Jack-in-the-Box-Inc.-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Earnings); [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2974453/jack-q3-earnings-top-estimates-revenues-miss-as-same-store-sales-fall)
- **Shake Shack:** It saw a weak start to 2026 [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-12/shake-shack-s-weak-sales-set-the-stage-for-a-challenging-2026), then same-Shack sales of +3.5% in Q2 2026 [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/shake-shack-q2-earnings-call-highlights).
- **Value and premium pricing:**
  - McDonald's runs $5 meal deals and new under-$3 and under-$4 tiers, and Burger King runs $5 and $7 menus. [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/lifestyle/mcdonalds-goes-all-in-affordability-full-menu-revealed-new-under-3-4-deals); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mcdonalds-burger-king-face-whopper-170617254.html)
  - At the same time, premium launches (McDonald's Big Arch, an upgraded Whopper) are under way. [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/27/mcdonalds-big-arch-burger-bk-whopper-refresh?stream=top)
- **Smash-burger saturation:**
  - Honest Burgers closed its Smash + Grab smash concept after about 18 months, and UK media frame the market as testing saturation (November 2026, UK). [Restaurant Online](https://www.restaurantonline.co.uk/Article/2026/11/19/smash-or-crash-inside-the-uks-smash-burger-market/)
  - San Diego's Smash and Stack closed in September 2025 after a short run. [San Diego Ville](https://www.sandiegoville.com/2025/10/smash-and-stack-smash-burger-eatery.html)
  - Expansion continues at 7th Street and Skinny Louie (see section 1).
- **GLP-1 drugs:**
  - Cornell found an about 8% drop in limited-service spending in a user's first year (December 2024).
  - Circana found items per visit fell only 1%, with mains favored over sides.
  - Redburn projected up to 28M fewer McDonald's visits.
  - [CTV News](https://www.ctvnews.ca/business/article/ozempic-and-other-glp-1s-about-to-take-a-bite-out-of-the-fast-food-business-experts/); [Entrepreneur](https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/weight-loss-drugs-are-changing-fast-food-restaurant-menus/502019)
  - A 2026 Toast survey of 850 users found 51% eat restaurant meals less often, with fewer fried foods. [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/data/glp-1-restaurant-habits)
  - Shake Shack launched a lettuce-wrap "Good Fit Menu".

### Inferences
- **Pricing position:** An independent cannot win on price against $5 meal deals. It must win on quality and experience at a $14 to $18 check, which is where better-burger chains are holding up.
- **GLP-1 effect:** GLP-1 drugs lean against fries and sides more than mains. That favors protein-forward items and smaller "treat" portions, so a single cinnamon roll suits this better than a large dessert.

### Gaps
- **No hard saturation data:** No US data was found on independent smash-burger openings and closures, and no Datassential or Technomic smash-burger penetration figures.
- **No Wendy's Q3 2026 release:** Wendy's Q3 2026 results were not confirmed from the company's release.

## 8. Equipment and build costs

### Takeaway
Budget about:
- $2.4K to $11K for a 36-inch gas griddle
- $35K to $80K installed for a full Type I hood exhaust package with suppression and make-up air
- $5K to $20K for fire suppression alone
- $3K to $15K installed for a grease trap

A second-generation space with a working hood and trap can save most of the $50K to $100K for those items.

### Cited Findings
- **36-inch gas griddles:**

  | Model | Price | Seller |
  |---|---|---|
  | Vollrath GGMDT-36 countertop | $2,379.30 | [WebstaurantStore](https://www.webstaurantstore.com/vollrath-ggmdt-36-cayenne-36-flat-top-gas-countertop-griddle-thermostatic-control/922GGMDT36.html) |

  - A Type I hood is about $20K to $50K, and a full exhaust package (suppression, make-up air, fans, ductwork) about $35K to $80K installed.
  - Other quotes run $950 to $1,200 per linear foot, or $1,200 to $2,800 per foot including ductwork.
  - [Toast](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog/on-the-line/restaurant-hood-installation-costs); [Restaurant Warehouse](https://therestaurantwarehouse.com/blogs/restaurant-equipment/commercial-kitchen-hood-installation-cost)

- **Grease trap:**
  - Installed cost is about $3K to $15K. Plumbing for a 50 to 100 GPM in-ground trap is $3K to $8K, and an above-ground unit $1.5K to $3K.
  - Pumping runs $150 to $400 a visit, or $600 to $4,800 a year.
  - Source: Pulse RevOps, low reliability (a future-dated "2027" title). [Pulse RevOps](https://pulserevops.com/knowledge/bo0154)
- **Full kitchen:** $120K to $350K depending on scope (vendor blog). [Projul](https://projul.com/blog/construction-commercial-kitchen-buildout-guide)

### Inferences
- **Second-generation savings:** In a second-generation burger space, re-certifying an existing hood, suppression and trap might cost $5K to $20K (cleaning, re-charge, inspection, minor duct work) instead of $50K to $100K new. This is an inference, not a sourced quote.
- **Bakery add-on:** Adding a bakery means a convection oven and proofer, and possibly a deck oven and mixer. Under most codes these do not need a Type I hood unless the equipment makes grease, but local code governs.

### Gaps
- **Not found:**
  - No fryer price (40-lb gas fryer, for example Pitco or Frymaster) was captured.
  - No Miami-specific build or permit cost data.
  - No sourced cost for re-certifying a second-generation hood.
- **Reliability:** Most hood and trap figures come from vendor blogs.
