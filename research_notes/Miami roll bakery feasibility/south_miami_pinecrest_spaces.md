Evidence note for the report writer. Every finding below comes from web-search result text, marked **(SRT)**. No listing page was read directly. Fetches to www.commercialcafe.com, www.loopnet.com and www.bizbuysell.com failed with "getaddrinfo ENOTFOUND", meaning those hosts are outside this session's allowed network. Following the tool's guidance, no other sites were fetched. The search tool's summaries sometimes merge several result pages. Where a figure could not be tied to a single URL, the bullet says "attribution uncertain". "Days old" is listing-age metadata shown in search results, counted from an unknown crawl date. "Last seen" means the listing appeared in search results on Nov 19, 2026, which does not prove it is still live. All 38 allotted searches were used.

## 1. South Miami: 2025–2026 asking rents by street, and the Shops at Sunset Place redevelopment

### Takeaway
Published South Miami asking rents for small shops cluster at **$60–$75/SF/yr base**:
- Sunset Drive: $60
- SW 73 St: $70
- Red Road: $75
- One Sunset Drive building: $75 NNN

The low outlier is $38/SF gross on SW 73 St. No South Miami listing quoted an NNN/CAM dollar amount.

Shops at Sunset Place has its approvals and won a county financing district in Oct 2026, but it had not been demolished by mid-Nov 2026. It is losing tenants now (Barnes & Noble is closing). Its planned ~150,000 SF of restaurant-heavy retail will be new construction, which means first-generation shells, with the first phase targeted for 2029. So in the near term it removes small food space rather than adding any.

### Inferences
- Illustrative base rent for a 930 SF bay, computed from the quotes above:
  - At $60/SF: $55,800 a year, about 10.5% of projected sales of ~$530,000.
  - At $75/SF: $69,750 a year, about 13.2%.
  - Both figures are before NNN. The 633 SF bay at $70 would be $44,310 a year (8.4%).
  - If the unverified $8–$15/SF CAM range applies, it adds $7,440–$13,950 a year on 930 SF.
- The $38 gross quote on SW 73 St (which includes taxes and insurance) is far below the $60–$75 base quotes. It may be stale or below market; verify before using it.
- Sunset Place:
  - Near term, it removes food space (tenants leaving, Barnes & Noble closing in Nov 2026).
  - Long term, it adds a large amount of restaurant space, but as new construction. That means shells, which the client excludes, and not before the 2029 first-phase target.
  - Construction may disrupt access and parking near Sunset Dr and US-1 (speculative).
  - The densification around the site (AvalonBay towers, residential units) is a longer-term demand positive for a downtown South Miami bakery.
- 1549 Sunset Dr does not fit South Miami's address numbering on Sunset Dr (the 5700–6100 blocks). It is probably in Coral Gables; not verified.

## 2. Pinecrest: 2025–2026 asking rents, owners, anchors, parking and visibility on the US-1 (Pinecrest Parkway) corridor and at the Killian and SW 136 St nodes

### Takeaway
Published Pinecrest asks range from **$34/SF** (a 13,001 SF box) and **$45/SF** (7,980 SF) to **$55/SF NNN** (4,900 SF at Pinecrest Center). One ~1,500 SF turnkey restaurant asks $8,500/month, which works out to about $68/SF a year (my calculation). Most centers show only "contact for pricing," and none disclosed NNN/CAM amounts.

The strongest co-tenancy and traffic counts are:
- Pinecrest Place (Regency): Whole Foods plus an adjacent Target, 95,000 VPD.
- Pinecrest Town Center: 64,500–76,000 VPD; sold Dec 5, 2025 for $42.5M.

Dadeland Plaza (Gator Investments) markets several second-generation restaurant units. Suniland's owner was not confirmed.

## 7. What landlords ask of a first-time independent tenant (lease length, deposit, personal guarantee, percentage rent)

### Takeaway
Miami-Dade brokers writing in 2026 say landlords expect a personal guarantee from first-time and single-unit restaurant operators, and increasingly want "proof of concept."
- Deposits run from 1–2 months' rent (generic guidance) up to 12–18 months in Miami Beach.
- Key money appears on turnkey spaces: $50,000 at 12257 S Dixie Hwy.
- Terms seen in listings: 10-year (Coral Gables), 3-year (Grove), and a lease assignment running to 2034.

No percentage-rent clauses were found.

### Cited Findings
**Guarantees and proof of concept**
- "Personal guarantees: especially for first-time restaurant operators or single-location tenants"; "Proof of concept: financial statements from existing locations, not just a business plan" — [Atlantic Commercial Advisors, Miami-Dade 2026](https://atlanticcommercialadvisors.com/blog/restaurants-for-lease-miami-dade-county-2026) (SRT).
- In Miami Beach, landlords "often want a personal guarantee on top of the corporate lease, even from established groups," and are "asking for 12-18 months of rent as security deposits" — [Atlantic Commercial Advisors, Miami Beach 2026](https://atlanticcommercialadvisors.com/blog/restaurants-for-lease-miami-beach) (SRT).
- Guarantees can be softened with burn-down clauses, good-guy clauses, or caps that limit exposure "to a fixed dollar amount rather than the full lease term." A good-guy guaranty "does not end the lease, and the tenant still owes the remaining rent" — [Delancey Street](https://www.delanceystreet.com/article/can-you-negotiate-a-personal-guarantee/); [Reichard Tornes](https://reichardtornes.com/restaurant-lease-agreement-guide/); [Jimmy Carey CRE (Atlanta-focused)](https://www.jimmycareycommercialrealestate.com/post/restaurant-tenant-representation) (SRT; attribution among these uncertain).
- A larger security deposit or a letter of credit can reduce or replace a personal guarantee, especially for tenants with strong financials (SRT; attribution among the same sources uncertain).

**Deposits**
- Deposits are "often the first month or two of rent," but landlords sometimes "require large security deposits." Tenants can negotiate a refund after a period without default — [Saddleback BBQ blog](https://www.saddlebackbbq.com/blogs/blog/lease-terms-restaurant-tenants-need-to-understand) (SRT; generic, not Miami-specific).
- Two actual Miami leases (older; office and other uses): one required a deposit plus first month's rent at signing; a 2019-era lease required first month, last month and a deposit at execution — [Justia: 40 SW 13 St lease](https://contracts.justia.com/companies/ealixir-inc-15923/contract/268742); [Justia: 350 NE 60 St lease](https://contracts.justia.com/companies/motorsport-gaming-us-llc-11698/contract/220970) (SRT).

**Lease length**
- One broker suggests "a short-term pop-up lease (6-12 months) in a food hall or shared kitchen space to build comps and cash flow before you sign a 10-year lease in a strip center" (SRT; one of the 2026 Miami broker blogs, possibly [Breadless franchise blog](https://franchise.eatbreadless.com/blog/miami-restaurant-franchise-investment-2026/); attribution uncertain).
- Terms seen in listings (SRT):
  - 10-year lease (Coral Gables restaurant, We Sell Restaurants 21342)
  - "3-year lease term" (near the Grove, We Sell Restaurants 23573)
  - A renewal option on the 1,350 SF unit at 12303 S Dixie Hwy (CommercialCafe)
  - "Negotiable," NNN (Dadeland Plaza, Crexi)
  - A café sale whose lease runs "until 2034" (South Beach, BizBuySell)

**Key money**
- "$50,000 Key Money Required" at 12257 S Dixie Hwy — [Fausto Commercial](https://faustocommercial.com/property/turn-key-2nd-generation-restaurant-in-pinecrest/) (SRT).
- A "Key Money Lease Assignment" is listed in Coconut Grove — [TenantBase](https://www.tenantbase.com/miami-fort-lauderdale/coconut-grove/retail-storefront/) (SRT).

### Inferences
- Illustrative deposits on a 930 SF bay at $60 base ($4,650/month before NNN; computed):
  - At 2 months: about $9,300.
  - At Miami Beach-style 12–18 months: $55,800–$83,700.
- As a first-time independent operator with no existing locations, the client should budget for a personal guarantee and possibly a larger deposit, and should negotiate a burn-down.
- Key money on turnkey spaces is a direct claim on the $500,000 cash budget and belongs in each pro forma as an up-front cost.
