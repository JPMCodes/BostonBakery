## 1. What are 2025–2026 asking rents (base and NNN/CAM) by street, and are there named lease deals with rent per sq ft?

### Takeaway
Small street-level retail in downtown Coral Gables asks about **$40–$47/SF/yr NNN** on the cheaper Ponce de Leon and US-1 strips. The only published CAM figure is **$14.50/SF (estimated)** at Ponce Plaza. Giralda and Paseo de la Riviera on US-1 ask about **$75/SF**, and Miracle Mile asks **$65–$95/SF** (Terranova, April 2026). The Miami-Dade average is $44–$50/SF. No 2025–2026 lease on any target street disclosed a per-SF rent. NNN/CAM charges are almost never published.

### Inferences
- Rent ladder (asking base rent, $/SF/yr):
  - About $40–$47: Ponce Plaza, Santona Corner.
  - $45: US-1 strips at 451–473 and 455 S Dixie.
  - About $75: 262 Giralda, Paseo de la Riviera and 219/221 Miracle Mile, roughly the bottom of Terranova's range.
  - $85: 1586 S Dixie (freestanding).
  - Up to $95: Miracle Mile per Terranova; Crexi's monthly Miracle Mile quotes would equal about $92–$118 a year if the units are right.
- Conversions (calc):
  - $4.71/SF/MO = $56.52/yr; $8.48/SF/MO = $101.76/yr; $9.09/SF/MO = $109.08/yr; $7.68–$9.83/SF/MO = $92.16–$117.96/yr.
  - The city pop-up's $2,200/mo on 850 SF = $31.06/SF/yr.
  - Terranova's $56,396,408 ÷ 96,791 SF ≈ $583/SF of total contract value over the lease terms. That mixes office, retail and a land lease, and cannot be turned into an annual rate without the term lengths.
- Occupancy cost for a 930 SF bakery on about $530,000 of sales (calc):
  - At Ponce Plaza terms ($40 base + $14.50 CAM = $54.50/SF): about $50,685/yr, about 9.6% of sales.
  - At Giralda's $75 base: $69,750/yr, about 13.2% before NNN.
  - At Miracle Mile's $65–$95 base: $60,450–$88,350/yr, about 11.4%–16.7% before NNN.
- NNN/CAM for the pro forma: Ponce Plaza's $14.50/SF is the only retail CAM found. Terranova's $24/SF office taxes, insurance and maintenance is the only Miracle Mile operating-cost figure. It could serve as a rough upper bound for older Miracle Mile buildings, but that is unverified.

### Inferences
- The gap between the 2–2.2% submarket vacancy (which counts shopping centers) and visible Miracle Mile vacancies suggests some Miracle Mile owners are holding out for high rents or credit tenants; the 1586 S Dixie owner states such a preference. An independent bakery may get better terms from smaller owners (Ponce Plaza, Santona Corner sublessors) or through a second-generation resale than from Terranova.
- Rising countywide supply and negative absorption in Q3 2026 should give tenants slightly more leverage into 2027. Free rent and TI should be requested even though no Coral Gables-specific figures were found.
