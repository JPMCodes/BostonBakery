> **How this was gathered (read first).**
> - **Scope and budget.** Compiled 2026-11-05 to fill the Gaps sections of `heritage_italian_and_specialists.md`, `asian_latin_immigrant_bakeries.md` and `chains_franchises_wholesale_channels.md`. Their earlier queries were not repeated. All **28 of 28** allotted web searches were used, and no search hit a quota or limit error.
> - **Fetches failed.** Two page fetches were tried: `bovabakeryboston.net` and `menupages.com`. Both failed DNS resolution because the hosts are outside this session's allowed network domains, so no further fetches were attempted. To check first-party prices, those two hosts would need to be allowed. So would the proxy-blocked `mikespastry.com`, `modernpastry.com` and `toasttab.com` noted in the sibling files.
> - **Every number below is extract-based.** It was read from a search engine's extract of the cited URL, not on the page itself.
> - **Dates.** Every row was observed on 2026-11-05. "Source date" is the page's own date where the extract showed one.
>
> **Grades (as the brief requires):**
> - **Hard-1P**: an actual listed price on the merchant's own ordering page, online store or press release.
> - **Hard-DEL**: an actual listed price on a delivery app or delivery-powered storefront (DoorDash's `order.online`, Uber Eats, Grubhub/MenuPages). These are real prices, but usually marked up compared with the shelf.
> - **Owner claim**.
> - **Press**.
> - **Aggregator**: third-party menu-price sites, directories, reviews and social posts. Low reliability.
> - **Estimate**: my own calculation.
>
> Rows tagged **(prior notes)** repeat figures from the sibling files, with their original sources, so each matrix is complete in one place. They were not re-searched.

## 1. Bagels: single bagel, bagel + cream cheese, bacon-egg-cheese, lox sandwich and dozen (Bagelsaurus, Exodus, Rosenfeld's, Black Sheep, Kupel's, PopUp, Brick Street, Mamaleh's, Pavement)

### Takeaway
The biggest bagel hole, sandwich prices, is now partly filled:
- **Mamaleh's** nova lox sandwich: **$13.75** on its Toast menus (Cambridge and Brookline) and **$15.00** on its own ordering site.
- **Pavement**: Build Your Bagel **$5.50**; named bagel sandwiches **$6.75–$11.00** (Toast).
- **Bagelsaurus**: a reviewer puts a sandwich with bacon at about **$10**.

For comparison, Bagelsaurus runs $2.00–$2.75 and PopUp $3.50–$5.00, the latter including a schmear (prior notes). No item prices surfaced for Kupel's, Black Sheep, Brick Street or Exodus. Brick Street is expanding: a headline reports a third Boston location coming to Beacon Hill.

### Inferences
- **Sandwich price ladder, independent and café bagel shops (Estimate from Table 1A):**
  - plain bagel: ~$1.50–$2.75
  - bagel with cream cheese: ~$4.50
  - build-your-own: $5.50 (Pavement)
  - specialty: $6.75–$9.00
  - egg or bacon: ~$10–$11
  - lox: $13.75–$15.00
  - Reviewers' totals with add-ons reach $14.99.
  - A Mamaleh's lox sandwich costs **5.0–5.5×** a Bagelsaurus single bagel ($13.75–$15.00 vs $2.75). Sandwiches, not bagels, carry the ticket.
- **Independents vs. Panera.** Egg and bacon bagel sandwiches at ~$10–$11 sit about **25–38% above** Panera's national $7.99 Asiago Bagel Stack promo (section 5).
- **Bulk pricing.** Ranked by per-bagel price:
  - Rosenfeld's: $1.50 (3 dozen)
  - Bagelsaurus: $2.00 (baker's dozen), $2.50 (half-dozen), $2.75 (single)
  - PopUp: $3.50–$5.00 (schmear included)

  Rosenfeld's bulk price undercuts even Panera's single-bagel aggregator range ($1.69–$2.49; section 5). Its "value" image in reviews therefore rests on bulk pricing (Estimate).
- **Mamaleh's two channels.** The $15.00 on its own site vs $13.75 on Toast is a **+9%** gap. Either a recent price rise has not reached every menu yet, or the channels are priced differently. It is unresolved.
- **Catering is common.** Mamaleh's, Kupel's, Brick Street and PopUp (prior notes) all list on ezCater. Kupel's ($125) and Brick Street ($70) set ezCater order minimums, which points to office catering as a meaningful revenue line for bagel shops. This is an inference; no revenue share was found.

### Gaps
- **No item prices found:** Kupel's (Uber Eats listing exists, but no price was extracted); Black Sheep; Brick Street (singles, spreads, sandwiches); Exodus. Exodus's 2025–26 operating status is still unconfirmed. PopUp's current 2026 pack prices (only the Feb 2025 figures exist).
- **Bacon-egg-cheese not found** at Mamaleh's, Rosenfeld's, Kupel's, Black Sheep, Brick Street or Exodus. **Lox sandwich not found** at Bagelsaurus, Rosenfeld's, Kupel's, Black Sheep, Brick Street or Pavement.
- Pavement's "Sunrise" fillings were not captured, so it may not be a bacon-egg-cheese.
- Brick Street's second unit and its Beacon Hill opening date were not captured.

## 2. Donuts: single and dozen prices at Union Square Donuts, Blackbird, Kane's and Greedy Cow; Dunkin' Boston baseline (glazed, dozen, bacon-egg-cheese, medium latte)

### Takeaway
**Blackbird** has the only clean premium-donut price ladder: **$3.50 a doughnut, $21 a half-dozen, $42 a dozen**. Toast and an aggregator agree, and there is no volume discount. **Union Square Donuts** is listed at **$4–$5 a donut** (Time Out Market listing, undated). Kane's dozen price and all Greedy Cow donut prices are still missing. Kane's runs a "Super Dozen" promotion: 15 donuts for the price of 12. Greedy Cow **reopened its storefront in Aug 2026**.

For **Dunkin'**, the Boston evidence is structural rather than a price list:
- Franchisees set their own prices.
- Logan Airport stores charge more.
- A 2024 Pioneer Institute survey of 40 Boston-area stores found prices loosely track local food-service density.

The only Boston-store latte price is **$3.89 for a medium (2019 and 2022)**. No Dunkin' bacon-egg-cheese price was found.

## 6. Brief: Watertown Armenian bakeries (Sevan, Massis, Arax), Haitian patties (Le Foyer) and Chinatown mooncakes
