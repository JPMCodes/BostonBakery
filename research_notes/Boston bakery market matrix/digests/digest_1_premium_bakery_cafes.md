**Menu inferences ([EST]):**
- File A: Tatte and Flour daily sales lean on savory breakfast, lunch and beverages ($16 BLTs, shakshuka ≈$19.55 on delivery apps, $4.50–$6.60 lattes); independents (La Saison, Michette, Lakon, Clear Flour, Bakey) rely on laminated/enriched pastry at $5–$9, center on ≈4–6 laminated items plus bread, often close by 2–4 pm (Michette, Imagine, Lakon Brookline/Newton) and add lunch only at a larger second shop. Holiday programs are a real business line for Flour and Petsi (Flour runs a catalog per event: Thanksgiving 2025, Mother's Day 2026, Thanksgiving 2026). Hero items get spin-offs (Sofra morning-bun ice cream; Flour sticky-bun packs, kit, 8-pack) and act as marketing: Lakon cube, Michette cardamom bun, Bakey Broissant are croissant-dough "format innovations" carrying a price premium.
- File B: Tatte is a café-restaurant first — ≈17 of 38 observed items are cakes, tarts, savory plates; only 4–5 viennoiserie; beverages and plates (shakshuka $14–$15) probably drive much of the ticket, consistent with ≈$1.5–1.7M per café. Likely Tatte signature lines: nut tart in two formats ($6.75 slice, $7.50 petit box); shakshuka in four flavors + GF + a sweatshirt (unconfirmed). Flour: ≈15 of 42 daily items are viennoiserie/quick breads; the ≈13-SKU, four-size pre-order catalog is a second business line; bread is minor (three loaves $7.50–$8.00, no baguette); its chocolate pastry is brioche, not laminated. Specialists run 5–15 visible items around one hero; savory arrives with bigger second sites. [EST, low confidence] true menus are likely meaningfully larger, especially drinks; no basis for a multiplier.
