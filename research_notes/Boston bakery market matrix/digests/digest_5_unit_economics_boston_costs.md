- **Inf-A butter sensitivity:** butter ~60% of croissant ingredients × CME butter −29% YTD 2026 → ingredient cost ~17% lower (0.60 × 0.29), worth ~1–6% of a $6 price; labor and waste matter more at retail, butter more in wholesale.
