### Inferences

**Dadeland Plaza is the strongest lease lead on size.**
- The 830 SF and 1,004 SF units bracket the client's ideal of about 900 SF.
- The three food tenants at 9471 S Dixie Hwy marked closed on Yelp pages updated in 2026 suggest at least one small bay there was recently fitted for food.
- A smoothie bay usually has hand sinks, a three-compartment sink and often floor sinks. A grease trap and a hood suitable for ovens are less typical. This is inferred from typical smoothie fit-outs and is unverified.
- Whether the 830 SF NNN unit is one of the closed bays was not found.
- Apamate Bistro still operates at 9471 (Aug 2026). It may have taken one of the closed bays (unverified).

**Palmetto Bay bays that match on size but not (yet) on food use.**
- The 900 SF bay at 14101–14115 S Dixie Hwy and the 1,000 SF bay at 15365–15375 S Dixie Hwy are the right size.
- Neither is shown as a former food space. Unless a broker confirms prior food use, drains and a grease trap, they are retail shells and fall under the client's exclusion.

**South Miami and Coral Gables south of Bird Road produced no in-range food bay.** Their fitted spaces are about 2,000 SF or more (5850 Sunset Dr, 400 S Dixie Hwy, 1586 S Dixie Hwy).

### Inferences

**Palmetto Bay versus Pinecrest**
- Small bays on US-1 cost about the same on either side of SW 136 St ($48–53 NNN at Shops at Palmetto Bay versus $45–55 NNN in Pinecrest).
- Palmetto Bay is clearly cheaper only at older or more southern centers (Palmetto Bay Center at $30, basis unstated) and at the low end of the 14101–14115 range ($35).

**Kendall**
- Asks in 2005-era community centers ($30, Kendall Village Center) run about $15–25 below Pinecrest. Downtown Dadeland ($60 NNN) runs above it.
- The $32/SF base implied by the 2,585 SF Kendall restaurant lease is consistent with the $30 Kendall Village Center figure.

**Fitted restaurant space carries a premium over plain retail.** The two Pinecrest fitted examples (12257 S Dixie at $68/SF plus key money; the Town Center diner at $66/SF) sit above Pinecrest's $45–55 retail NNN asks. Both may be gross figures, which is unknown.
