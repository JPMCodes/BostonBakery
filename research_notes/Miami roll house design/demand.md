## 1. Guava and cheese: who sells a guava-and-cheese roll or pastry beyond the classic pastelito, at what price and size, and how popular

### Takeaway
A guava-and-cream-cheese cinnamon roll already exists in Miami, but only from small sellers: Roll Model (pre-order boxes picked up at Merrick Park in Coral Gables), the home bakery Casa Canela ($30 a half-dozen, $54 a dozen) and Rolled near FIU (2023; whether it still operates is unknown). No established bakery publishes a guava-roll price or a best-seller claim. The guava-and-cheese pastries with documented sell-outs are laminated ones, chiefly Casa Bake's guava-and-cheese croissant in South Miami. The Cuban baseline is about 48 cents a piece for a mini guava pastelito bought by the platter.

### Inferences
- **The flavour is proven in Miami but not unique.** At least three cinnamon-roll sellers have offered guava and cream cheese, so the roll must stand out through execution, size and occasion rather than novelty.
- **Roll Model keeps its specialty flavours off pre-order.** That suggests small makers keep fillings off the pre-order list to keep production simple; this is my reading, not a stated reason. The client should decide whether the guava roll goes into boxes or stays a walk-up and market item.
- **Casa Bake proves demand inside a target town.** A premium guava-and-cheese pastry sells out in South Miami, and heavy demand pushed that bakery to online pre-orders (Q2).
- **Price tension.** A guava roll at roughly $4.50–$10 sits beside a 48-cent mini pastelito and a $1.89 full-size one. The premium works for a boxed treat or gift, not as the everyday guava fix.
- **Verdict on the guava-and-cream-cheese roll:** supported as a flavour. The evidence of popularity is press and listicle praise plus sell-outs of laminated guava pastries. There are no sales figures.

### Inferences
- **Box sizes of 4, 6 and 12 match what Miami already buys.** Crumbl sells 4/6/12, Cinnabon 4/6, Roll Model 2/4/6, and Knaus the half-dozen and dozen.
- **The per-unit price ladder (my arithmetic):**
  - Knaus: about $1.45 a roll at the farm.
  - Knaus resold in Miami: about $2.67–$2.85 a roll on delivery apps, and $3.33 shipped by Goldbelly.
  - Crumbl: about $3.67–$4.29 a cookie in a 12-pack, and $4.12 in a 4-pack.
  - Cinnabon: about $4.75–$6.62 a roll in a 4-pack.
  - Keik: $8.99–$9.99 for a single roll.
- **Crumbl's volume discount is small:** about 11% from the 4-pack to the cheapest 12-pack.
- **Convenience earns a premium.** Resellers charge roughly 84–97% over the farm price for a Knaus 6-pack delivered in Miami (my arithmetic, $16.00–$17.11 for 6 against $1.45 a roll). Some buyers will pay to avoid the drive to Redland, which helps a closer pickup point.
- **Knaus's own model is a ready template:** a quantity cap, pickup days released a month ahead, and a 7:30–10:30 a.m. pickup window.
- **Verdict on boxes of 4, 6 and 12 by online pre-order for pickup:** supported. Casa Bake in South Miami went online-only after selling out, Roll Model pre-sells boxes with pickup at Merrick Park in Coral Gables, and Knaus is adding advance orders. Three caveats:
  - Neither small operator publishes volumes.
  - Roll Model limits pre-orders to one flavour.
  - Casa Bake's "plan a couple of days ahead" suggests pre-order-only selling can lose impulse buyers.

### Inferences

- **Verdict on Thursday–Sunday mornings (Friday–Sunday in summer):** consistent with local practice, but the evidence is thin and anecdotal.

### Inferences
- **The Coral Gables market is an 11-week winter test** (January to March, Saturdays). If 2027 follows 2026, it would probably start in mid-January, with applications through the special events office. The deadline is not published, so the client should contact the city now.
