The menu's planning pars (`par_daily` in each product) add up to **$3,628 of sales a day**, or $1,305,900 a year over 360 days, with food and packaging at 20% of sales at distributor prices and 23.4 hands-on prep hours. Each site's `sales_index` scales that day to the site, and each scenario scales it again. Year 1 ramps from 55% to 100% of the steady state, averaging 80%; Year 3 adds 3% of price growth. ## Other assumptions

- **Days and hours:** 360 days a year, 6:30 am to 3:00 pm, seven days.
- **Variable costs, % of sales:** card processing 2.6%, repairs maintenance 1.5%, supplies cleaning 1.5%, marketing 2.0%, delivery net 0.5%, miscellaneous 1.0%.
- **Fixed costs a year:** insurance $8,500, software pos $5,000, accounting payroll legal $9,000, licenses music $2,500, waste pest $7,200, linens uniforms $2,400, internet phone $2,400.
- **Utilities:** $10 a sq ft a year.
- **Inflation:** wages, NNN and fixed costs rise 3% a year; base rent rises at each site's escalation.
- **Before opening:** rent and NNN are paid from lease signing to opening, for each site's months to open; no free rent is assumed.
- **Not included:** income taxes, depreciation, owner's draw beyond the manager line, and any landlord build-out allowance or free rent, which would lower the startup cost.
