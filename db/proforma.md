## How sales are projected

Each format has a design day: its products' daily volumes times their prices. The bakery-café uses every product's `par_daily`; the roll bakery uses the short menu and volumes listed under its format in `proforma.yaml`. A site's `sales_index` scales its format's design day to the site, and each scenario scales it again. The order counts divide the items sold by an assumed items per order, so treat them as rough. Year 1 ramps from 55% to 100% of the steady state, averaging 80%; Year 3 adds 3% of price growth. Food cost is the menu's cost at distributor prices plus 5.5 points for waste beyond the recipes' allowances, comps, staff meals and price drift.

Break-even is the daily sales at which Year-2 cash flow after debt service is zero. Payback is the startup cost divided by Year-3 EBITDA. The most base rent a site can carry is the rent at which Year-2 cash after debt service falls to zero, or, for an owner-run format, to the owner's target pay, with its NNN unchanged; an assumed rent is a placeholder until a broker quotes one. The site scan's rent test is base rent at or under 8% of sales; NNN charges come on top. Year-1 cash after debt has to fit inside the working capital reserve.

## Other assumptions

- **Days and hours:** bakery-café 360 days a year, 6:30 am to 3:00 pm, seven days; roll bakery 307 days a year, 7:00 am to 2:00 pm, Tuesday to Sunday.
- **Variable costs, % of sales:** card processing 2.6%, repairs maintenance 1.5%, supplies cleaning 1.5%, marketing 2.0%, delivery net 0.5%, miscellaneous 1.0%.
- **Fixed costs a year:** insurance $8,500, software pos $5,000, accounting payroll legal $9,000, licenses music $2,500, waste pest $7,200, linens uniforms $2,400, internet phone $2,400. Totals by format: bakery-café $37,000; roll bakery $27,300.
- **Utilities:** $20 a sq ft a year.
- **Inflation:** wages, NNN and fixed costs rise 3% a year; base rent rises at each site's escalation.
- **Before opening:** rent and NNN are paid from lease signing to opening, for each site's months to open; no free rent is assumed.
- **Not included:** income taxes, depreciation, owner's pay in an owner-run format, owner's draw beyond the manager line in a managed one, and any landlord build-out allowance or free rent, which would lower the startup cost.
