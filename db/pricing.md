The cost range runs from the distributor estimate to the retail estimate. Packaging is costed for to-go orders; served on a plate for here, it drops out. Margins exclude labor, rent, card fees and utilities; the research puts a typical Boston bakery at 3–9% net profit after those. Both products sit far below the 25–35% food-cost benchmark in the report, so the market and labor set these prices, not cost-plus: at a 30% food-cost target the roll would price at $1–$2.

- **The roll's box is its biggest cost.** At distributor prices the box ($0.32) costs more than the roll's food ($0.29), and packaging is over half the roll's cost. A 6×6 sugarcane-fiber clamshell (about $0.15 at WebstaurantStore) would halve the packaging, at some cost to presentation.
- **The sandwich label does a job:** the two sandwiches look alike once wrapped, so the label names which is which and seals the wrap.
- **Not costed:** a box for the 4-pack, and bags for multi-item orders.

## Blueberry Scone: $4.50

| Boston comparable | Counter price |
|---|---|
| Flour scones | $4.50–$4.75 |
| Flour muffins | $4.50–$5.00 |

- **At par with Flour's scones,** and a quarter under the cinnamon roll, which stays the hero.
- **Sour cream instead of crème fraîche saves $0.21–$0.28 a scone** ($2.45–$3.26 a batch). Crème fraîche runs about $26–$30/kg even by the case; sour cream about $5–$7/kg. Adding 25 g butter and taking out 25 g buttermilk keeps fat and liquid within about 2 g of the crème fraîche version. Total cost drops from 26–38% of the price to 20–33%.
- **Fresh blueberries cost about $0.07–$0.12 a scone** across the dough and the topping, and more out of season.
- **Dried blueberries are the biggest ingredient line,** at $0.17–$0.32 a scone.
- **Packaging** is the same window box as the cinnamon roll; check that the baked scone fits.

## Breakfast Sandwiches: $8.50, or $10.25 with fennel sausage

| Boston comparable | Counter price | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Flour kids' breakfast egg sandwich | $7.00 | Kids' portion; build not listed |
| Panera Asiago Bagel Stack | $7.99 | National 2026 promo price |
| Tatte halloumi sunny-side | $9.85 | Fried egg, halloumi, spinach |
| Flour breakfast egg sandwich | $10.00 | Focaccia, ham or bacon, cheddar, arugula, tomato, dijonnaise |
| La Saison Egg Bun | $11.00 | Egg, avocado, tomato, bacon, Dijon |

- **Below the bakery-café band on purpose:** Boston bakery breakfast sandwiches run $9.50–$11.00, but each carries meat, greens or halloumi. This one is egg, cheddar and harissa on a house roll, so it sits $1.35–$2.50 under them and above the kids' and chain-promo tier.
- **Ticket:** with a latte ($4.50 at Flour, $4.85 at Tatte) the check is about $13, near Flour's $14.50 egg-sandwich-plus-latte check.
- **Fennel sausage SKU at $10.25:** $1.75 above the base sandwich. With meat on it, it joins the $9.50–$11.00 band: a quarter above Flour's $10.00 ham-or-bacon egg sandwich and $0.75 under La Saison's $11.00 Egg Bun. The extra quarter over $10.00 is pure margin, and with Boston's 7% meals tax it still rings up under $11 ($10.97). The 2 oz patty costs $0.36–$0.69. One reviewer reported paying $3 extra for sausage at Pavement, the high end of add-on pricing.
- **Why it matters:** breakfast sandwiches are Tatte's two most-liked DoorDash items, and in the one Boston bakery-café sales mix the research found, pastry and desserts were only 27% of sales.

Flour's drink prices are from its Boston Common ordering page and its drip and iced coffee from the market research; Flour doesn't state cup sizes. Tatte's chai prices are from Toast listings.

- **Match the market on drinks:** the research found Boston's premium lives in the bake, not the cup: lattes overlap from the chains to the top independents ($3.89–$6.00). So these sit at Flour and Tatte's level, a quarter up where our cup is bigger (iced latte) or the matcha is premium.
- **Quality shows in the cost, not the price:** at distributor prices, drinks cost 20–33% of their price, against 10–13% for the bakery items with their packaging. The coffee is the biggest line: about $13–$14 a pound wholesale for specialty beans.
- **Weakest margin is the chai:** 33% at distributor prices, because a premium concentrate costs about $1.12 a drink. A house chai of black tea, whole spices and sugar, like Tatte's, would cut that by more than half.
- **Drip assumes dumping 15% of every batch** at the 60-minute hold. Selling through faster lowers the cost.
- **Espresso is costed to go.** For here, in ceramic, it's about $0.10 cheaper.
- **Not costed yet:** alternative milks and syrups.

## Rules carried over from the research

- Benchmark counter prices only. Delivery apps run 10–40% above the counter in Boston. If these go on apps, list them about 20% higher ($5.70, $10.20 and $12.30) and stay on the lowest-commission plan.
- Plan to hold these prices through 2026–27. Butter (CME cash $1.28/lb on Nov 4, 2026) and eggs ($2.27 a dozen in Sept 2026) are well below their peaks.
- Revisit once real distributor quotes replace the estimates in `ingredient_prices.csv`, or after the first month of sales.
