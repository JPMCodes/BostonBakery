# Retail pricing: approved products

Suggested counter prices, set 2026-11-06. Benchmarks come from the Boston market research in [`reports/Boston bakery market matrix.md`](../reports/Boston%20bakery%20market%20matrix.md) (counter prices mostly seen Nov 5, 2026) and its notes. Ingredient costs come from [`costing.md`](costing.md).

| Product | Suggested price | Ingredient cost | Ingredients plus $0.25 packaging, as % of price | Margin per unit after ingredients and packaging |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cinnamon Roll (4 oz, buttermilk glaze) | **$4.75** | $0.29–$0.64 | 11–19% | $3.86–$4.21 (81–89%) |
| Breakfast Sandwich (egg, cheddar, harissa) | **$8.50** | $0.71–$1.44 | 11–20% | $6.81–$7.54 (80–89%) |

The cost range runs from the distributor estimate to the retail estimate. The $0.25 packaging figure is the one the research uses in its channel economics. Margins exclude labor, rent, card fees and utilities; the research puts a typical Boston bakery at 3–9% net profit after those. Both products sit far below the 25–35% food-cost benchmark in the report, so the market and labor set these prices, not cost-plus: at a 30% food-cost target the roll would price at $1–$2.

## Cinnamon Roll: $4.75

| Boston comparable | Counter price | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Paris Baguette and Tous les Jours buns | $3.35–$4.40 | National listings, not Boston shelf prices |
| Michette cardamom bun | about $4.40–$4.90 | Research estimate of the in-store price, backed out of its $5.75 DoorDash listing |
| Flour and Tatte plain croissant | $4.50 | |
| Flour sticky bun | $5.25 | Caramel-pecan bun; about 600 a day chain-wide in 2019 |
| Sofra morning bun | $6.00 | Tagged "popular" on Uber Eats |
| La Saison kouign-amann | $6.00 | Laminated |
| Flour sticky bun 4-pack, pre-order | $22 | Mother's Day 2026 ($21 at Thanksgiving 2025) |

- **Under the pecan bun:** $0.50 below Flour's sticky bun, which carries caramel and pecans. A glazed cinnamon roll is the simpler item.
- **Over the croissant and the chain buns:** a quarter above the $4.50 plain croissant at Flour and Tatte, for a bigger 4 oz item, and above the $3.35–$4.40 chain buns.
- **Option:** a 4-pack box at $18 for pre-orders and gifting. That's $4.50 a roll, about 5% off four singles and $4 under Flour's 4-pack.
- **Later:** move to $5.00 if it sells out daily, or at a prime-rent site. The research shows Tatte prices by location.

## Breakfast Sandwich: $8.50

| Boston comparable | Counter price | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Flour kids' breakfast egg sandwich | $7.00 | Kids' portion; build not listed |
| Panera Asiago Bagel Stack | $7.99 | National 2026 promo price |
| Tatte halloumi sunny-side | $9.85 | Fried egg, halloumi, spinach |
| Flour breakfast egg sandwich | $10.00 | Focaccia, ham or bacon, cheddar, arugula, tomato, dijonnaise |
| La Saison Egg Bun | $11.00 | Egg, avocado, tomato, bacon, Dijon |

- **Below the bakery-café band on purpose:** Boston bakery breakfast sandwiches run $9.50–$11.00, but each carries meat, greens or halloumi. This one is egg, cheddar and harissa on a house roll, so it sits $1.35–$2.50 under them and above the kids' and chain-promo tier.
- **Ticket:** with a latte ($4.50 at Flour, $4.85 at Tatte) the check is about $13, near Flour's $14.50 egg-sandwich-plus-latte check.
- **Sausage add-on:** +$1.50, for $10.00. That matches Flour's $10.00 breakfast egg sandwich with ham or bacon and stays under La Saison's $11.00 Egg Bun. A 2 oz sausage patty costs about $0.35–$0.65, so the margin after ingredients and packaging is $7.66–$8.69 (77–87%). One reviewer reported paying $3 extra for sausage at Pavement, the high end. Needs its own approval and costing first.
- **Why it matters:** breakfast sandwiches are Tatte's two most-liked DoorDash items, and in the one Boston bakery-café sales mix the research found, pastry and desserts were only 27% of sales.

## Rules carried over from the research

- Benchmark counter prices only. Delivery apps run 10–40% above the counter in Boston. If these go on apps, list them about 20% higher ($5.70 and $10.20) and stay on the lowest-commission plan.
- Plan to hold these prices through 2026–27. Butter (CME cash $1.28/lb on Nov 4, 2026) and eggs ($2.27 a dozen in Sept 2026) are well below their peaks.
- Revisit once real distributor quotes replace the estimates in `ingredient_prices.csv`, or after the first month of sales.
