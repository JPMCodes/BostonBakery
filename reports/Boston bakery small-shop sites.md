# Boston Bakery Small-Shop Sites: One of Five Pays Without the Owner Baking

*Checked November 10, 2026. This applies the roll bakery format in [`db/proforma.yaml`](../db/proforma.yaml) to small former food spaces: the cinnamon roll, six other pastries and coffee, about 930 sq ft, no griddle or lunch. The owner manages the shop but a hired lead baker does the baking, and the shop is paid for in cash with no loan (both decided November 10). It swaps the grease-hood requirement of the [site scan](Boston%20bakery%20site%20scan.md) and the [site shortlist](Boston%20bakery%20site%20shortlist.md) for "prior food use". Most listing facts are search-result snippets, and nothing was confirmed with a broker.*

Five small former food spaces are in the pro forma, but with a hired lead baker and no loan only one clears the bar on estimated sales: the former Chip City cookie bakery at **1 Brattle Sq, Harvard Square**, which leaves the owner about $67,000 a year, a 24% return on $279,000 of cash. It is not on the market and its rent is a guess. The other four leave $22,000–$51,000 a year, 6–12% on $383,000–$519,000: a **Jamaica Plain café for sale** ($52,800 a year all-in, $95,000 for the fit-out), **24 College Ave by Davis Square** ($41,400 a year, but the $200,000 asked for equipment puts it $19,000 over the owner's $500,000 limit), a **Brighton Center juice bar** ($26,400 a year, lease to 2034, next door to Lakon) and **6 Church St** in Harvard Square (rent unpublished). At sales 20% lower all five lose a little or about break even. The hired baker is the difference: about $77,000 a year with payroll taxes, more than the $30,000–$57,000 of loan payments that paying cash saves. Hingham Square and an East Somerville café were modeled and dropped.

## What the shop needs

- **Orders.** On the format's design day the shop takes about 195 orders at about $8.86, including about 140 rolls. With a hired lead baker the sites below break even at 168–227 orders a day and pay the owner $60,000 at 214–273.
- **The owner's role.** The owner manages for about six hours a day and does not bake; the costs include no pay for the owner, and $60,000 is what a hired manager would cost instead. Hiring a manager too would take about $69,000 a year more, which no site here covers. With the owner at the bench instead of a hired lead baker, the same sites broke even at 146–199 orders.
- **Occupancy.** Before rent and owner's pay, a 930 sq ft shop has about $61,000 left at 195 orders a day, $112,000 at 234 and $151,000 at 263. Each 20 more orders a day adds about $27,000.
- **The space.** A former café, bakery, dessert shop or small restaurant, with food-service plumbing, a restroom and a recent food permit. Raw retail is out. The shop bakes in electric ovens and has no griddle or fryer, so it should not need a grease hood; the local building and health departments decide that.
- **Capital.** $279,000–$519,000 to open, all of it the owner's cash; the owner's limit is $500,000.

### Notes by site

1. **1 Brattle Sq.** Chip City's cookie bakery from 2024 until the chain shut every store on October 2, 2026. The 2023 permit drawings show a double electric convection oven, three reach-in freezers, a three-bay sink, a grease trap and an accessible restroom, which would make it the cheapest space to open if they are still there. It can carry up to about $114 a sq ft of base rent and still pay the owner $60,000. Risks: it is not listed, and who controls the lease and equipment is unclear; the rent is a guess; Flour is about two minutes away and Tatte about four. Owner Beacon Capital; leasing by KeyPoint Partners (snippet).
2. **24 College Ave.** An operating 12-seat quick-service restaurant steps from the Davis Square Red Line entrance and Tufts, live on the market since August 29. No Tatte or Flour in Davis; J.P. Licks is at 4 College Ave and Yafa Bakery is coming to 187 Elm St. Risks: only 600 sq ft at street level, so baking moves to the basement if the city allows it; the $200,000 asked buys equipment a bakery mostly can't use and takes the total to $519,000, over the owner's limit (at $75,000 the total falls to $394,000 and the return rises from 10% to 13%); a new lease has to be negotiated.
3. **6 Church St.** Taiyaki NYC and The Dough Club (mochi donuts and ice cream) from 2022 to November 2025, beside the Red Line station and across Mass Ave from the campus. Listed with no rent. Risks: the rent is a guess and has to come in under about $90 a sq ft for the owner to make $60,000; empty for a year; fit-out unknown; Tatte is three to four minutes away and Flour about five.
4. **Jamaica Plain café.** A working café "for coffee, pastry, baked goods" with 12 seats, sold with its fit-out and equipment; the listing puts it in the heart of JP with heavy pedestrian traffic and a municipal lot nearby. No Tatte or Flour in Jamaica Plain (Flour on Tremont St is 1.4 miles away); the center has Café Selah, Cafe Siete, Caffè Nero, Monumental Market and Viza Cafe. Risks: the broker withholds the address; the lease ends June 30, 2027 with one 5-year option, short for a 10-year loan; sinks, grease trap and power are not stated.
5. **Brighton Center.** A franchise juice bar since November 2023; the city licence shows a prep area, 23 seats, a bathroom and basement storage. The cheapest rent found and the longest lease (to July 5, 2034). Lakon Paris Patisserie is next door at 407 Washington St; Cafenation, Sunday Bakery and Starbucks are within a quarter mile. Risks: the landlord and the franchisor must accept a new concept; 705 sq ft leaves room for about four seats; no ovens, power unknown. On the estimated sales it can't pay the owner $60,000 at any rent.

- **Not on the market, or status unclear:** 292 Concord Ave, Cambridge (Nine Winters Bakery, closed March 2026); 15 Spencer St, West Newton (Lavender Café, closed August 2025); 5 Bellevue St, West Roxbury (gelato shop still licensed); 361 Huron Ave, Cambridge (unclear which half is vacant); 6-12 Cypress St #12, Brookline Village (600 sq ft plus basement at $38,400, a year-old listing); Chip City's other shops at 97 Causeway St, 316 Newbury St and Arsenal Yards; 277 Harvard St and 1378A Beacon St in Coolidge Corner; Lola's Coffee, West Medford; Mass Hole Donuts, Teele Square; NU Kitchen, Union Square.
- **Taken:** the Jamaica Plain When Pigs Fly (now Café Selah); Home.stead in Fields Corner (Bottega Cannuccia); 570 Tremont St (Brick Street Bagels); 359 Huron Ave (Sumi House).
- **Nothing small and food-ready found** on Charles St, in Newton Centre, Savin Hill, Ashmont, Lower Mills, South Boston, Charlestown or East Boston, or in Concord, Winchester, Milton, Belmont Center, Watertown Square, Medford Square, Legacy Place, Assembly Row or the Porter, Inman, Central, Kendall, Union, Ball and Magoun squares.

One caution from the closures: Weesh Bake Shop in Roslindale stopped walk-in sales in June 2026 after five years, saying retail had not been profitable, and Lavender Café lasted under four months on a hidden West Newton side street.

## Before calling brokers

- **Everyone:** is it still available; base rent, last year's NNN, term and options; what stays (three-bay sink, hand and mop sinks, grease trap, restroom, counter); electric service in amps and whether it is three-phase; whether convection ovens without a grease hood, take-out and early-morning baking are allowed.
- **Jamaica Plain café:** the address; a lease beyond 2027; why it is for sale; the equipment list.
- **24 College Ave:** the landlord's terms for a new lease without the $200,000 of equipment; whether the basement can be used for baking.
- **Brighton Center:** whether the landlord and franchisor will permit a bakery; whether $2,200 a month is gross; the asking price.
- **1 Brattle Sq:** whether Chip City's lease has ended; whether the oven, freezers and sinks stay; panel size and phase; the rent.
- **6 Church St:** whether the 850 sq ft unit is the old Dough Club space; the rent; what was left behind.

## Method and caveats

- **How it was searched.** boston.com's listings (which mirror the MLS) for 33 ZIP codes, Boston Restaurant Talk's posts since May 2025, Boston's licence and inspection data, and about 120 web searches. LoopNet, Crexi, CityFeet, BizBuySell and BizQuest block automated reading, so their facts are search-result snippets; realmo.com and universalhub.com could not be read.
- **Sales levels are judgment calls.** Harvard Square is put at 1.35 times the format's design day, Davis Square 1.15, Jamaica Plain 1.1 and Brighton Center 0.95. The break-even and target order counts don't depend on them.
- **Estimates in the model.** Build-out costs, the value of equipment left behind and the two Harvard Square rents are assumptions until a contractor and a broker weigh in.
- **Nothing was checked with a broker or landlord,** and no space was visited. No shortlisted space has confirmed ovens or three-phase power.
