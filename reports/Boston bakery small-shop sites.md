# Boston Bakery Small-Shop Sites: Five Former Food Spaces for a Roll Bakery

*Checked November 10, 2026. This applies the roll bakery format in [`db/proforma.yaml`](../db/proforma.yaml) to small former food spaces: the cinnamon roll, six other pastries and coffee, about 930 sq ft, owner-run, no griddle or lunch. It swaps the grease-hood requirement of the [site scan](Boston%20bakery%20site%20scan.md) and the [site shortlist](Boston%20bakery%20site%20shortlist.md) for "prior food use". Most listing facts are search-result snippets, and nothing was confirmed with a broker.*

Five spaces are worth a call, and each is in the pro forma. Three have published numbers: a **Jamaica Plain café for sale** ($52,800 a year all-in, $95,000 for the fit-out), **24 College Ave by Davis Square** ($41,400 a year, but the seller wants $200,000 for equipment a bakery mostly can't use) and a **Brighton Center juice bar** ($26,400 a year, lease to 2034, next door to Lakon). Two are in **Harvard Square**, where traffic is highest and rent is unpublished: the former Chip City cookie bakery at 1 Brattle Sq, which may still have its oven and sinks but is not on the market, and 6 Church St. On estimated sales the five leave the owner $58,000–$128,000 a year after loan payments; at sales 20% lower, $4,000–$51,000. Hingham Square and an East Somerville café were modeled and dropped.

## What the shop needs

- **Orders.** On the format's design day the shop takes about 195 orders at about $8.86, including about 140 rolls. The sites below break even after loan payments at 146–199 orders a day and pay the owner $65,000 at 190–243. The costs include no pay for the owner.
- **Occupancy.** At the design day the shop can carry about $36,000 a year of rent and charges and still pay the owner $65,000; each 20 more orders a day adds about $30,000 of room.
- **The space.** A former café, bakery, dessert shop or small restaurant, with food-service plumbing, a restroom and a recent food permit. Raw retail is out. The shop bakes in electric ovens and has no griddle or fryer, so it should not need a grease hood; the local building and health departments decide that.
- **Capital.** $262,000–$503,000 to open, 30% of it the owner's.

### Notes by site

1. **Jamaica Plain café.** A working café "for coffee, pastry, baked goods" with 12 seats, sold with its fit-out and equipment; the listing puts it in the heart of JP with heavy pedestrian traffic and a municipal lot nearby. No Tatte or Flour in Jamaica Plain (Flour on Tremont St is 1.4 miles away); the center has Café Selah, Cafe Siete, Caffè Nero, Monumental Market and Viza Cafe. Risks: the broker withholds the address; the lease ends June 30, 2027 with one 5-year option, short for a 10-year loan; sinks, grease trap and power are not stated.
2. **24 College Ave.** An operating 12-seat quick-service restaurant steps from the Davis Square Red Line entrance and Tufts, live on the market since August 29. No Tatte or Flour in Davis; J.P. Licks is at 4 College Ave and Yafa Bakery is coming to 187 Elm St. Risks: only 600 sq ft at street level, so baking moves to the basement if the city allows it; the $200,000 asked buys equipment a bakery mostly can't use (at $75,000 the owner would keep about $14,000 a year more); a new lease has to be negotiated.
3. **Brighton Center.** A franchise juice bar since November 2023; the city licence shows a prep area, 23 seats, a bathroom and basement storage. The cheapest rent found and the longest lease (to July 5, 2034). Lakon Paris Patisserie is next door at 407 Washington St; Cafenation, Sunday Bakery and Starbucks are within a quarter mile. Risks: the landlord and the franchisor must accept a new concept; 705 sq ft leaves room for about four seats; no ovens, power unknown.
4. **1 Brattle Sq.** Chip City's cookie bakery from 2024 until the chain shut every store on October 2, 2026. The 2023 permit drawings show a double electric convection oven, three reach-in freezers, a three-bay sink, a grease trap and an accessible restroom, which would make it the cheapest space to open if they are still there. Risks: it is not listed, and who controls the lease and equipment is unclear; the rent is a guess; Flour is about two minutes away and Tatte about four. Owner Beacon Capital; leasing by KeyPoint Partners (snippet).
5. **6 Church St.** Taiyaki NYC and The Dough Club (mochi donuts and ice cream) from 2022 to November 2025, beside the Red Line station and across Mass Ave from the campus. Listed with no rent. Risks: the rent is a guess and has to come in under about $136 a sq ft for the owner to make $65,000; empty for a year; fit-out unknown; Tatte is three to four minutes away and Flour about five.

## Checked and dropped

- **Too big or too dear:** 57 Lincoln St, Newton Highlands (2,200 sq ft at $45, about $99,000 base); 627 Tremont St, South End (1,800 sq ft, rate undisclosed); 1948 Beacon St, Cleveland Circle (1,859 sq ft, rent on request; worth asking whether it can be split); the Lexington Center café ($78,756 a year) and the Harvard Square café ($91,788 base) for sale; 315–317 Chestnut St, Needham ($72,000 before NNN); 68A Central St, Wellesley (SALT Patisserie is announced there).
- **Next to a Tatte:** 1038 Beacon St, Brookline (850 sq ft shell, former TBaar); 1853 Mass Ave, Lexington Center (850 sq ft, former Abbott's; the owner blamed low sales).
- **No kitchen left, or no food history:** 349 Centre St #A, Jamaica Plain (1,200 sq ft at $47,400, renovated as office space); 1383 Washington St, West Newton; 14 Green St #101, Coolidge Corner; 51 Holland St, Somerville; 59 Dartmouth St, South End.
- **Not on the market, or status unclear:** 292 Concord Ave, Cambridge (Nine Winters Bakery, closed March 2026); 15 Spencer St, West Newton (Lavender Café, closed August 2025); 5 Bellevue St, West Roxbury (gelato shop still licensed); 361 Huron Ave, Cambridge (unclear which half is vacant); 6-12 Cypress St #12, Brookline Village (600 sq ft plus basement at $38,400, a year-old listing); Chip City's other shops at 97 Causeway St, 316 Newbury St and Arsenal Yards; 277 Harvard St and 1378A Beacon St in Coolidge Corner; Lola's Coffee, West Medford; Mass Hole Donuts, Teele Square; NU Kitchen, Union Square.
- **Taken:** the Jamaica Plain When Pigs Fly (now Café Selah); Home.stead in Fields Corner (Bottega Cannuccia); 570 Tremont St (Brick Street Bagels); 359 Huron Ave (Sumi House).
- **Nothing small and food-ready found** on Charles St, in Newton Centre, Savin Hill, Ashmont, Lower Mills, South Boston, Charlestown or East Boston, or in Concord, Winchester, Milton, Belmont Center, Watertown Square, Medford Square, Legacy Place, Assembly Row or the Porter, Inman, Central, Kendall, Union, Ball and Magoun squares.

One caution from the closures: Weesh Bake Shop in Roslindale stopped walk-in sales in June 2026 after five years, saying retail had not been profitable, and Lavender Café lasted under four months on a hidden West Newton side street.

## Before calling brokers

- **Everyone:** is it still available; base rent, last year's NNN, term and options; what stays (three-bay sink, hand and mop sinks, grease trap, restroom, counter); electric service in amps and whether it is three-phase; whether convection ovens without a grease hood, take-out and early-morning baking are allowed.
- **Jamaica Plain café:** the address; a lease beyond 2027; why it is for sale; the equipment list.
- **24 College Ave:** the landlord's terms for a new lease without the $200,000 of equipment; whether the basement can be used for baking.
- **Brighton Center:** whether the landlord and franchisor will permit a bakery; whether $2,200 a month is gross; the asking price.
- **1 Brattle Sq:** whether Chip City's lease has ended; whether the oven, freezers and sinks stay; panel size and phase; the rent.
- **6 Church St:** whether the 850 sq ft unit is the old Dough Club space; the rent; what was left behind.

## Method and caveats

- **How it was searched.** boston.com's listings (which mirror the MLS) for 33 ZIP codes, Boston Restaurant Talk's posts since May 2025, Boston's licence and inspection data, and about 120 web searches. LoopNet, Crexi, CityFeet, BizBuySell and BizQuest block automated reading, so their facts are search-result snippets; realmo.com and universalhub.com could not be read.
- **Sales levels are judgment calls.** Harvard Square is put at 1.35 times the format's design day, Davis Square 1.15, Jamaica Plain 1.1 and Brighton Center 0.95. The break-even and target order counts don't depend on them.
- **Estimates in the model.** Build-out costs, the value of equipment left behind and the two Harvard Square rents are assumptions until a contractor and a broker weigh in.
- **Nothing was checked with a broker or landlord,** and no space was visited. No shortlisted space has confirmed ovens or three-phase power.
