# Miami Roll House: Four Mornings a Week, Rolls by the Box

The bakery can work in Miami if it sells the cinnamon roll the way Miami already buys it: as a weekend event, by the box and by pre-order, picked up from a parking lot. The redesign, called the roll house here, opens Thursday to Sunday mornings (Friday to Sunday in summer), takes about 41% of its sales in boxes of 4, 6 and 12 and take-and-bake packs, adds a guava-and-cheese roll, runs an iced-first coffee bar, prices at Miami specialty levels and drops the scones, muffins and coffee cake. In the pro forma it leaves you **$61,712 in Year 2 on $411,242 of cash at Dadeland Plaza's 1,004 sq ft unit, against $8,641 for the Boston-style roll bakery in the same space**, and $76,408 on $307,857 in the center's 830 sq ft unit if that turns out to be a former food bay. It breaks even at 166 orders an open day and pays you $60,000 at 213, against 215 estimated. It rests on one unproven number: **at half the box volume it loses $12,737 a year**. So the plan starts with three to six months of market stalls and online pre-orders from a commissary, with the lease signed only when the box share and Miami prices hold.

## What changes, and why

The Boston roll bakery failed in Miami because it asked Miami to behave like Harvard Square: about 195 walk-in orders at $8.86, six mornings a week, all year. The best Miami spaces got Brighton-to-Jamaica Plain traffic at higher rents, so the same shop left $8,641 at Dadeland Plaza. Miami's buying habits point somewhere else. People wait in line for Knaus Berry Farm's rolls from October to May and buy them by the dozen, with a four-dozen limit and pre-orders of 4–12 dozen this season ([WLRN](https://www.wlrn.org/business/2026-10-01/knaus-berry-farms-cinnamon-rolls)). Keik sells its $8.99–$9.99 roll on weekends only, with lines around the block ([Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/food-drink/miami-is-obsessed-with-a-new-cinnamon-roll-but-expect-long-lines-22854991/)). Casa Bake in South Miami sells out of guava-and-cheese croissants by mid-morning and moved to online orders ([Miami New Times](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/food-drink/miami-is-obsessed-with-this-bakerys-croissants-but-expect-a-line-23727467/)). Suburban trade peaks on weekends, and owners report summer dips of 20–40% ([Axios, 2025](https://www.axios.com/local/miami/2025/07/30/miami-restaurants-summer-slowdown-2025); [Axios, 2026](https://www.axios.com/local/miami/2026/05/28/miami-restaurants-summer-slowdown-closures)).

In our judgment the roll house competes for the weekend treat and the gift box, not the morning cafecito, and the price ladder ($5.25 alone, about $4.17 a roll in a dozen) is what makes the box the better buy.

The four new products are in the database as drafts: the [guava-and-cheese roll](../db/products/guava_cheese_roll.yaml) and its [filling](../db/formulas/filling_guava_cheese.yaml) (the signature dough, a sweet cream-cheese layer and diced guava paste, a lighter buttermilk-glaze drizzle), the [6-box](../db/products/cinnamon_roll_6box.yaml), the [dozen](../db/products/cinnamon_roll_12box.yaml) and the [take-and-bake six-pack](../db/products/take_and_bake_6.yaml) (shaped, frozen unproofed in a foil pan, with a glaze cup and instructions). None has been baked. Food and packaging come to 14.9% of the design day's sales at distributor estimates, before the model's 5.5-point buffer.

## How it runs

The bakers shape the afternoon before and hold the rolls overnight in the proofer-retarder, so each morning starts at 5 am with baking, glazing and boxing against the pre-order list. Wednesday is a closed prep day for dough, fillings and take-and-bake packs. You manage and sell the boxes, catering and school orders; you don't bake. Paid hours average 43.5 an open day at Miami wages:

| Role | Hours an open day | Miami wage | Covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lead baker | 10 | $23.00 | 5 am bake and glaze, then tomorrow's dough and shaping; 40 hours a week with the Wednesday prep day |
| Baker and prep | 9.5 | $19.00 | Pre-order boxing, cookies and brownies, fillings, take-and-bake |
| Barista | 7 | $16.00 | Iced-first bar; plus tips |
| Counter | 9 | $15.25 | One on Thursday and Friday, two at the weekend; register and pickup shelf |
| Peak help | 5 | $15.00 | Weekend pickup rush and the line |
| Dish and close | 3 | $15.00 | |

*Model inputs: wages from the Miami cost block (Florida's minimum wage reached $15.00 on September 30, 2026), 11% payroll burden. The paid crew averages $17.93 an hour before burden.*

The menu database counts 22.7 hands-on hours for the design day, so the schedule has less slack than the Boston shop's: we judge it workable only because pre-orders fix most of the bake a day ahead and the shop is open six hours. If production runs slower, five more baker hours a day cost about $20,000 a year (see the stress tests).

Three readings, ours. The box share is the business: it is worth about $74,000 a year between the design and half of it, more than every other lever combined. Fewer days only pays if the demand concentrates: spreading the same yearly volume over a fifth day costs about $44,000, so add Wednesdays only when Thursdays sell out. And the low case is thin everywhere ($5K–$22K at the three sites), so the test has to prove the volume before a lease, not after.

## Who else is doing this

A Miami cinnamon-roll specialist with a storefront doesn't exist in the three towns, but the format has small precedents. **Roll Model** sells boxes of 2, 4 and 6 by online pre-order with pickup at Merrick Park in Coral Gables and at Jackson Memorial, makes a guava-and-cream-cheese roll (kept off pre-order), takes catering orders of 15 rolls or more and says "Baked at 5am · Sold till gone" ([Roll Model](https://www.rollmodelrolls.com/); [Roll Model menu](https://rollmodelrolls.lovable.app/menu)). **Casa Canela**, a home bakery, sells a guava roll by the half-dozen and dozen ([Bakesy](https://bakesy.shop/b/casa-canela-miami)). **Roll & Dough**, a pop-up, sells out at markets; a missed delivery once cost it about 300 rolls and $3,100 of a Saturday market's sales, the only published market-day figure in South Florida ([Pembroke Pines FL News](https://www.pembrokepinesflnews.com/lifestyle-entertainment/restaurants/article316989001.html)). In our judgment these confirm the demand and the price points, and Roll Model is the one to watch: it's the same idea without a shop, and it picks up at Merrick Park.

Miami's breakout bakeries all sold before they leased. The Salty Donut ran pop-ups for about a year before its 2016 shop, Zak the Baker sold at a farmers market for about two years, Night Owl delivered from home for about four, El Bagel sold from a car trunk and a truck, and Fireman Derek's made 50–100 pies a day from a warehouse kitchen before its 2014 shop ([Miami New Times, Salty Donut](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/food-drink/the-salty-donut-opens-permanent-wynwood-store-this-saturday-with-free-doughnuts-8955626/); [Zak the Baker](https://zakthebaker.com/about-us/); [Miami New Times, El Bagel](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/food-drink/el-bagel-delivers-fresh-bagels-in-miami-with-plans-for-wynwood-pop-up-9364434/); [Miami New Times, Fireman Derek's](https://www.miaminewtimes.com/food-drink/fireman-dereks-thanksgiving-pies-and-how-to-make-the-perfect-crust-6580364/)). Take-and-bake rolls sell elsewhere at $4–$7.50 a roll (Flour in Boston: $39 for eight), but no Miami bakery sells them now ([Flour Bakery](https://www.flourbakery.com/products/brown-butter-cinnamon-rolls-ready-to-bake?v=43381783134378)).

## The plan: test, then lease

**Stage 1, January to July 2027: sell before you sign.** Hire the lead baker part-time and bake from a commissary; CityChef in Miami starts at $300 a month plus hourly time ([CityChef](https://www.citychefcatering.com/membership)). The home-baking law allows up to $250,000 a year but likely rules out the dairy glaze, so the commissary is the safer route ([FDACS](https://www.fdacs.gov/Business-Services/Food/Food-Establishments/Cottage-Foods); [Cottage Food Law Library](https://cottagefoodlawlibrary.org/florida)). Sell three ways: the Coral Gables Saturday market at City Hall (January to March; 2027 dates and fees not yet posted; apply through the city's special events office), the Pinecrest Farmers Market on Sundays at Pinecrest Gardens (fees unconfirmed; one page lists a $100 application and $65 a week), and online pre-orders for Saturday pickup ([Coral Gables Gazette](https://gables-gazette.com/coral-gables-farmers-market-returns-to-city-hall/); [Pinecrest Farmers Market](https://pinecrestfarmermarket.com/)). Keep running through June and July to measure the summer. By our rough arithmetic outside the model, a part-time baker, the commissary, market fees, a tent and display, packaging and ordering software come to about $30,000–$45,000 over six months before sales.

**The gates to sign a lease.** Our judgment, tied to the model's thresholds:

| Gate | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Box share | At least 40% of roll sales in boxes and take-and-bake packs, at $19 / $27 / $50 / $24 | The design's 41%; half of it loses money |
| Prices | Sales hold at $5.25 a roll and $5.75 for guava, with no discounting | Boston prices cost about $23,000 a year |
| Volume | 300 or more rolls a market day by March, and 100 or more pre-ordered boxes a week by June | Roll & Dough's 300-roll market day; the shop needs about 156 boxes in a four-day week |
| Repeat | 40% or more of pre-orders from repeat customers; a list of 1,500 or more | Weekly habit, not a one-time event |
| Summer | June–July weekend sales at 60% or more of March's | The summer schedule assumes the dip, not a collapse |
| Guava roll | 25% or more of singles | It's in the design at 40 a day |

**Stage 2: the lease.** Sign only with the gates met, ideally for an 800–1,000 sq ft former food bay with parking at no more than about $55 base plus $12 NNN (the Dadeland Plaza units, the 9471 S Dixie bays or the Pinecrest café). Open in October 2027 with Knaus's season, with the test's list as the launch. **Stage 3**, the bigger shop with lunch from your original plan, comes once the roll house has a year of numbers.

## Risks

The volume is the risk. The design day asks for about 1,412 rolls a week from a strip-center bay, and no Miami bakery publishes its sales, so the number rests on the traffic research's ranking and on Knaus-, Keik- and Casa Bake-style lines. A pre-order-only habit can also lose impulse buyers, which is why the design keeps a walk-in counter. The guava roll is an untested recipe with a cheese layer that should sell the day it's baked. Roll Model could open a shop first, and Paris Baguette arrives across US-1 at Dadeland. Every site's rent is assumed or unconfirmed. On the other side, Miami's costs already help: Florida's wages and power save $16,000–$19,000 a year on the same shop, and the model includes that.
